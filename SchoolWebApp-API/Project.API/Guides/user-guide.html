Getting Started

ShuleNova is your school's management system: learners and parents, staff, classes, assessments and exams, teaching documents, fees and accounts, payroll, elections and messages to parents, in one place in your web browser. This guide shows how to do the everyday work. The screens shown use a demonstration school; your school's names and figures will differ.

What you see depends on you. The menus you see follow the roles your administrator gave you, and the modules your school has bought. If this guide describes a page you cannot find, ask your administrator to give you access.

Signing In

  1. Open your school's ShuleNova address in your browser (for example https://yourschool.kodetek.co.ke).
  2. Type your Username and Password. The eye button shows the password as you type it.
  3. Tick Remember me on your own computer only, then click Sign In.

The first time you sign in, or after an administrator resets your password, ShuleNova asks you to choose a new password before you continue.

Forgot your password? ShuleNova does not send reset emails. Ask your system administrator to reset it; you then choose your own at the next sign-in.

Finding Your Way Around

Every page has the same frame:

  • The menu on the left lists the areas you can use: School, Staff, Students, Academics and so on. Click an area to open its pages. The ☰ button at the top left hides the menu and gives the page more room.
  • The top bar holds, from the right: your account menu, the notifications bell, and the guides.
  • The page itself, with its title and the path back to the dashboard at the top right.

Most lists have a search box, filters such as year, term and class above them, and page controls below them. A page often remembers your filters in its address, so a refresh or a bookmarked link opens the same view again.

Notifications and Approvals

The bell shows the items waiting for you, most often requests to approve: an expense, a budget, a scheme of work or a lesson plan sent to you as the next approver. Click an item to open it, then approve or return it with a comment. Click Refresh to check for new ones.

Your Account and Password

Click the person icon at the top right to see your name, email and roles, and to change your password or sign out.

Click your name to open My Profile, where you can also change your password: type your current password, then the new one twice, and save. Staff paid through ShuleNova also find a My payslips button here.

Signing Out and Staying Safe

  • Always use Sign out in the account menu when you finish, above all on a shared computer.
  • If you leave ShuleNova idle, it shows an "Idle mode detected" warning with a countdown, then signs you out by itself (after 30 minutes unless your school chose otherwise). Click Resume to carry on, or Log out.
  • Never share your password. Everything you do is recorded against your name.
View-only accounts. Some accounts (a board member, an auditor) can look at everything but change nothing. They show a yellow View only badge in the top bar, and any save is refused with the message "This account is view-only".

School

The School menu holds the facts about your school: its details, its classes, the school calendar, and reports on classes and their attendance. Most staff only look at these pages; an administrator keeps them up to date.

Setting up the year. Academic Years, Sessions (terms) and creating the year's classes are set up by an administrator at the start of each year. They are described in the Administrator Guide.

School Details

School > Details shows the school's name, logo, contacts, motto, vision and mission, and the school levels it offers. The report header and titles shown here are what appear at the top of printed reports, such as report forms and class lists.

The fields are greyed out so that nothing changes by accident. Administrators see an Edit school details link at the top right: it opens the fields for changing (and lets you choose a new logo), and Update Records saves them. If something here is wrong, such as a phone number on your reports, tell your administrator.

Events (the School Calendar)

School > Events is the school calendar: opening and closing days, exams, sports days, trips and meetings. The dashboard's Upcoming events card shows the next ones from this list.

Looking at the calendar

  1. Open School > Events. The current academic year's events are listed, latest first.
  2. To see another year, pick it in the year box and click Search.

Each row shows the term (session) the event falls in, its name, place, start and end dates, and a description.

Adding or changing an event

If you are allowed to manage events:

  1. Click Add event at the top right.
  2. Choose the Academic year and the Session name (term), then type the Event name and Event location. These four are required.
  3. Set the Event start date and Event end date (the same date for a one-day event) and, if you like, a Description.
  4. Click Save.

To change an event, click the pencil in its Action column; the same form opens with its details. The bin deletes it after you confirm.

Tip. Administrators can also reach this page from the Add event link on the dashboard's Upcoming events card; a Back to dashboard button then takes you back.

School > Classes lists the year's classes: each grade and stream (for example Grade 5 E and Grade 5 W), with its class leaders and the number of learners in it.

Finding a year's classes

  1. Open School > Classes.
  2. Check the year in the first box. The page may open on the latest year set up, which can be next year's (still empty) classes; pick the year you want.
  3. Leave the curriculum as it is, or pick a Level (such as Junior School) to see only its classes, then click Search.

Seeing who is in a class

Click the blue number in the Students column. A window lists the class's learners with their UPI (admission number), gender, phone and status. Click Close when done.

Adding classes, copying last year's classes into a new year (Copy classes from another year) and editing a class are administrator tasks, covered in the Administrator Guide. Learners are placed in classes from Students > Class Assignment (see the Students chapter).

School Reports

School > Reports has three reports about classes. Each opens already filled in for the current year, so you see results straight away; change the filters and click Search to see something else. The filters you choose stay in the page address, so a refresh or a saved link shows the same report again.

Printing makes a PDF that opens in a new browser tab, from which you print or save it.

Class Lists

School > Reports > Class Lists gives the list of learners in each class, for registers, notice boards or exam rooms.

  1. Pick the year and the learners' status (normally Active; choose Alumni, Suspended or Terminated for those lists) and click Search. The year's classes appear on the left.
  2. Click a class's name to see its class leaders and learners on the right.
  3. To print, tick the box beside each class you want (the box in the heading ticks them all), then click Print List. With one class ticked the button reads Preview List.

Attendance Report

School > Reports > Attendance Report shows a class's register for a month on one screen: a row per learner and a column per day, with P (present, green) or A (absent, red). Grey days have no register taken, such as weekends and holidays.

  1. Pick the year, the class, the status (normally Active) and the month, and click Search. The page opens on the month of the class's latest register.
  2. Click Print for the whole class's monthly register, or the printer icon at the end of a row for one learner's.

Attendance Detailed

School > Reports > Attendance Detailed lists one learner's attendance day by day for the month, with remarks, which is useful when talking to a parent about absences.

  1. Pick the year, class, status and month and click Search. The class's learners appear on the left.
  2. Click a learner's name to see their days on the right: the date and weekday, Present or Absent, and any remark.
  3. To print, tick the learners you want and click Print (Preview when only one is ticked).
Where does attendance come from? These reports show what class teachers record in Students > Attendance. A blank day means no register was taken for it.

Subject Allocation

Students > Subject Allocation sets which subjects each learner takes. Exams and assessments only have a place for a learner's mark in the subjects allocated to them, so allocate subjects at the start of the year, before the first exam.

Seeing a class's subjects

  1. Pick the curriculum, year, level and class at the top. The class loads as soon as it is chosen.
  2. Each learner's row lists their subjects.

Allocating subjects

  1. With the class shown, click Add allocations.
  2. Tick the learners on the left (Select all for the whole class) and the subjects on the right.
  3. Click Save. Every ticked learner gets every ticked subject; subjects a learner already has are skipped.
Religious education and options. Give the whole class its common subjects in one go with Select all, then add each option (such as CRE or IRE) to just the learners who take it.

Removing subjects

Click the × on a subject to remove it from one learner, or tick learners and click Delete selected to remove all of their subjects. ShuleNova asks you to confirm first.

Removing a subject removes its marks. If the learner already has exam results in that subject, they are deleted with it. The confirmation tells you how many results will go; read it before you click Delete.

Attendance

Students > Attendance is the class register. Class teachers mark it each day; the attendance reports under School > Reports are built from it.

Taking the register

  1. Pick the curriculum, year, level and class, and the date (no later than today), then click Search. The page opens on the first class and the last day a register was taken.
  2. The class's learners are listed. On a new day nobody is ticked yet: tick Present? in the heading to tick everyone, then untick the learners who are absent. A learner left unticked is saved as absent.
  3. Type a reason in Remarks for an absent learner (for example "Sick - parent called"). Your school may require a remark for every absence; ShuleNova then won't save until each absent learner has one.
  4. Change Time In if a learner came late.
  5. Click Update Student Attendances at the bottom and confirm.

Searching a day that already has a register shows it as it was saved, so you can correct it and save again. The bin on a row deletes that learner's record for the day.

Tip. The class, date and other choices stay in the page address. Bookmark the page with your class chosen and it opens straight on your class each morning.

Class Assignment (Promotion)

Students > Class Assignment moves learners from one class to another, many at a time. Use it at the end of the year to promote a class into next year's class, or during the year to correct a learner's class or stream (for example from 5E to 5W).

Before promoting. Next year's academic year and classes must already be set up by an administrator.

Promoting a class to next year

  1. Under From (Current Class), choose this year and the class. Under To (New Class), choose next year and the class the learners go up to. ShuleNova suggests the next grade in the same stream.
  2. Leave Also remove from source class unticked, so the learners keep their record in this year's class.
  3. Click Load Students. The learners still to move are listed under Students to Assign.
  4. Tick the learners to promote (the box in the heading ticks them all). Leave unticked any learner repeating the year or leaving.
  5. Click Assign Selected and confirm.

Learners already in the new class are listed under Already Assigned, so you can promote a class in several sittings without doubling anyone.

Correcting a class during the year

  1. Choose the same year on both sides, the learner's current class under From and the right class under To.
  2. Also remove from source class ticks itself when both years are the same; leave it ticked.
  3. Click Load Students, tick the learners, and click Move Selected.

A move keeps the learner's subjects, attendance and results for the year: they follow the learner into the new class.

Undoing a promotion

Tick learners under Already Assigned and click Reverse Selected. This removes them from the new class; their record in the old class stays. Reverse only before anything (subjects, attendance or marks) has been recorded for them in the new class.

Staff Attendance

Staff > Attendance is the daily staff register, usually kept by the deputy head or the secretary.

  1. Choose the date (no later than today). Leave Staff category and Employment type empty for everyone, or choose one to list only those staff. Click Search. The page opens on the last day a register was taken.

Academics

The Academics menu is where teachers record how learners are doing: CBE assessment levels against each specific learning outcome, marks in the school's exams, and the learner-development records (co-curricular activities, responsibilities, community service and values) that appear on the report form. Its Reports pages turn all of this into broadsheets, report forms and performance analysis.

You find it under Academics in the menu, in four groups: Assessments, Exams, Learner Development and Reports.

Who can enter what. A teacher enters assessments and exam marks only for the subjects and classes they are allocated to under Staff > Subject Allocation. Administrators can enter for any teacher, class and subject. So can any role given Student Assessments (Admin) in Menu Permissions (a Director of Studies, for example). That menu item is a permission, not a page: clicking it opens Student Assessments.

Most pages here open already filled in: the current year and term, a first class and subject, with the list loaded. Change any filter and click the page's Load button to see something else. The filters are kept in the page's address, so a refresh or a bookmark brings back the same view.

Student Assessments

Student Assessments is where you grade learners against the curriculum's specific learning outcomes, one sub-strand at a time. Each grade is a CBE performance level such as EE (Exceeding Expectations), ME (Meeting), AE (Approaching) or BE (Below), with its points in brackets: EE (4). Your school chooses the scale (4-point, or 8-point with levels like EE1 and EE2) in its grading settings.

Entering assessments for a sub-strand

  1. Open Academics > Assessments > Student Assessments. The page opens on the current term and one of your classes and subjects, with the sub-strand you assessed last.
  2. Check the filters across the top: Curriculum, Year, Session (the term), Class, Subject, then Strand and Sub-Strand. A Theme filter appears for subjects whose design groups strands into themes.
  3. On the second row choose the Assessment Type (for example Formative Assessments) and the Teacher. A teacher sees only their own name here. The Date is optional.
  4. Click Load Assessments. The grid lists every learner in the class, with one column per specific outcome in the sub-strand.
  5. Pick a level in each cell. Leave a cell on - if you have not assessed that outcome yet.
  6. Click Save All Assessments at the bottom. Only new or changed cells are sent. Saved cells turn green.
  • The header shows how many learners are listed and how many grades are saved. Use the search box to find a learner by admission number or name.
  • The Average row at the bottom shows the class's typical level for each outcome.
  • The small red × beside a saved grade deletes that one grade. Delete All Saved removes every saved grade in the grid, after a confirmation.
Not allocated? If you pick a class and subject you do not teach, a yellow warning says "You are not allocated to this subject for the selected class" and the save button is disabled. Ask your administrator to allocate the subject to you under Staff > Subject Allocation.
Tip. Grade as you go: after each sub-strand is taught, load it and enter the levels. The Assessment Tracker counts a sub-strand as due a week after the last scheme-of-work week that teaches it.

Assessment Tracker

The Assessment Tracker shows how far each teacher has got with formative assessments this term. It has one row per teacher, learning area and class. The scheme of work says which sub-strands are due for assessment, and the grades entered show which are done. Teachers see their own rows. A Head of Department sees their department(s) and their own learning areas. Administrators see the whole school. The line under the filters tells you which of these you are seeing.

How Approvals Work

Expenses, journal entries, budgets, budget amendments and credit or debit notes are not posted the moment they are saved. They go to one or more approvers first, in the steps your administrator set up under Settings > Approval Workflows. The form for each of these has an Approval box at the bottom.

  1. Fill in the form. In the Approval box, pick an approver for each step from the people who hold that step's role.
  2. Click Save & Submit for Approval. The record is sent to the first step's approver, who sees it under the bell at the top of the page. If you only click Save (no approvers picked), it stays a draft; open it again with the edit button and submit it when you are ready.
  3. The approver opens the record (the ✓✓ Review / Approve button in the list, or the link under the bell), reads it, writes a comment if they wish and clicks Approve, Return or Reject. A comment is required to return or reject.
  4. When the last step approves, the record takes effect: an expense or journal is posted to the ledger, an amendment changes the budget, a note changes the learner's invoice.
  • Returned records come back to you with the approver's comment shown at the top of the Approval box. Correct the record and click Resubmit for Approval.
  • Rejected records stop there. The comment says why.
  • Once submitted, a record is locked: the edit and delete buttons disappear. Depending on the school's setting, it stays editable until the first step approves. An approver can never edit or delete what they approve.
  • If the school switched on maker-checker for a workflow, you cannot pick yourself as an approver.
  • Only a Super Administrator can reverse an approval. The record then goes back to draft.
"No active workflow configured". If the Approval box shows this message, nobody can approve the record and it cannot be posted. Ask your administrator to add a workflow for that form.

Recording a payment

  1. Click New Payment.
  2. Under Find Student, choose the Academic Year (and Session if you like), the Class, then type a name or admission number in Student and pick the learner.
  3. Under Payment Details, choose the invoice in Apply to Invoice; each shows its balance. Leave it -- Unallocated -- only if the money is not for a particular invoice.
  4. Enter the Date, the Amount and the Method (Cash, M-Pesa, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Card or Other). Choose the Receiving Account the money went into, and type the M-Pesa code or bank slip number in Transaction Ref.

Correcting a payment: credit and debit notes

Payments are never edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a note, which goes through approval:

  • A credit note (the minus button) reverses all or part of a payment and puts the amount back on the invoice balance, for example a payment recorded against the wrong learner. It cannot be more than the payment.
  • A debit note (the plus button) adds to the invoice total, increasing what the learner owes, for example a bounced cheque.
  1. Click the credit or debit note button on the payment.
  2. Type the Amount and the Reason (both required).
  3. Pick the approvers and click Issue & Submit for Approval.

The note appears in the payments list with its own number, type and status, and changes the invoice only when it is approved.

Accounting Periods

At the end of each term (or each year, as the school chose) the books are closed: the period's income less expenses is moved to Retained Earnings, and nothing dated in it can be recorded, changed or deleted any more. Open Finance > Expenses & Accounts > Accounting Periods and choose the Academic Year.

  • the period before it is closed;

Payroll

ShuleNova pays your staff every month: it works out each person's gross pay, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, the Housing Levy, other deductions and loan instalments, prints the payslips and produces the returns and bank schedule. The monthly work is done in this order:

  1. Employee Salaries: who is paid, and how much.
  2. Loans & Advances: any loan or advance to recover from pay.
  3. Processing: create the month, process it, check the payslips, and send it for approval.
  4. Reports: once it is approved, print the payslips, muster roll, statutory returns, bank schedule and P9 cards.

All four are under Payroll in the menu. The rates and rules behind the calculation (tax bands, NSSF bands, reliefs and so on) are set up once by an administrator; see the administrator guide.

Who sees payroll. Payroll pages are for the people your school has made payroll officers. Every other member of staff sees only their own payslips, under My Payslips.

Employee Salaries

Payroll > Employee Salaries lists every active member of staff, one row each, with their basic salary, house, transport and other allowances, gross pay, the date the salary takes effect and whether it is active. The badges at the top count the staff with a salary, without one, and those excluded from payroll.

Setting or changing salaries

  1. Type the amounts straight into the row: Basic Salary, House Allow., Transport Allow. and Other Allow. The Gross column adds them up as you type, and a changed row turns yellow.
  2. Give each changed row an Effective Date. To use the same date for everyone listed, pick it in the Effective Date box at the top and click Apply to all.
  3. Click Save All. The number on the button shows how many rows will be saved. Discard throws away the changes you have not saved.

Use the Staff Member and Show filters (all, with salary, without salary, excluded from payroll) to narrow the list. A member of staff marked Not set has no salary yet and will not be paid until they have one.

Additional earnings and voluntary deductions

Some staff get more than the four standard amounts (overtime, a responsibility allowance) or have something taken off besides the statutory deductions (welfare, SACCO, union dues, an insurance premium). These are kept per employee:

  1. Click the list button (☰) at the end of the row. The Edit Employee Salary window opens.
  2. Under Additional Earnings click Add Earning, choose the Earning Type and type the amount. Do the same under Voluntary Deductions with Add Deduction.
  3. Check the Gross Total, Total Deductions and Net Salary at the bottom, then click Save.

The net salary shown here leaves out PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy; those are worked out when the payroll is processed. The number badge beside a name in the grid shows how many extra lines that person has.

A new employee. For someone with no salary yet, the window shows only the salary details. Save them first; the window then reopens with the earnings and deductions sections.

Statutory numbers and staff not on the payroll

A yellow warning above the grid counts the staff missing a KRA PIN, ID, NSSF or SHA number. Fix these before you process the payroll, or those lines print blank on the payslips and returns. Click the card button at the start of the row's actions (it shows a warning sign while something is missing) to open Payroll Details:

  • Fill in the KRA Pin, ID Number, NSSF No and SHA No and click Save. They are saved on the staff record.
  • Switch on Exclude from payroll for people on the staff list who are not paid through payroll, such as suppliers or contractors. They stay on the staff list but are skipped when the payroll is processed and get no payslip.

Names, staff category and employment details are changed on the staff record (Staff > Staff Details), not here.

Who gets paid. Processing pays every active member of staff who has an active salary and is not excluded. Someone whose employment date is after the end of the month is not paid for that month. The trash button deletes a salary record; the person stays on the list but will not be paid.

Loans & Advances

Payroll > Loans & Advances lists the loans and salary advances being recovered from pay, with the principal, the monthly deduction, the balance still owed and the status (Active, Fully Paid or Cancelled).

Recording a loan or advance

  1. Click Add Loan / Advance.
  2. Choose the Staff Member and the Issue Date, and leave the Status at Active.
  3. Type the Principal Amount and the Monthly Deduction (both must be more than 0) and the Balance still to recover, which for a new loan is normally the principal.

Elections

ShuleNova runs student elections from start to finish: the positions, nominations, the voter register, polling stations where students vote on a tablet, phone or computer, the live count and the declared results, which can then be recorded in the students' leadership records.

The Elections menu has three pages:

PageWho uses it
Manage ElectionsThe elections officers, who set elections up and run them.
Polling StationThe returning officers, usually class teachers, who let students vote at their station.
ResultsElections officers while polling is open; everyone with the page once the results are declared.

How an Election Runs

An election moves through six stages, in order. The elections officer moves it on with the green button at the top of the election's page.

StageButton to reach itWhat happens
Draft(new election)Set up the positions.
Nominations openOpen nominationsCandidates are nominated and approved. Polling stations are set up. Positions can still change.
Candidates finalClose nominationsThe approved candidates go on the ballot. A frozen voter register is taken now. Positions are fixed.
Polling openOpen pollingReturning officers issue ballots; the count shows live.
Polling closedClose pollingNo more ballots. Ties are settled by drawing lots.
Results declaredDeclare resultsThe leading candidates are elected; the results are final and visible to all staff.

Each move asks you to confirm and lets you add a note for the record. Some moves can be undone from the ⋯ menu: back to draft from nominations, Reopen nominations from Candidates final (this clears the ballot order and a frozen register, which are taken again when nominations close), and Reopen polling until the results are declared.

ShuleNova will not move on until the stage's work is done. It refuses to open nominations until every position has at least one race; to close nominations while a nomination still waits for approval or a race has no approved candidate; and to open polling until every class with voters has a polling station.

Manage Elections

Elections > Manage Elections lists the school's elections with their year, stage, number of positions and races, and the planned polling window. Filter by Year and Stage, or search by title. Click an election, or its Open button, to work on it.

Creating an election

  1. Click New election.
  2. Type the Name (for example "Student Council Elections 2026") and choose the Academic year. A Description is optional.
  3. Choose the Voter register:
    • Frozen: a snapshot of the voters taken when nominations close. A student who changes class afterwards votes where they were registered.
    • Live: who may vote follows each student's current class until they vote.
  4. Set Polling starts and Polling ends. This is the planned window shown to returning officers; polling itself opens and closes only when you move the stage.
  5. Set Results refresh (seconds), how often the live results screen updates, and click Save.

The election opens on its own page: its name, year, register type and polling window at the top, the stages it has passed, and a tab for each part of the work. A tab opens when the election reaches its stage (Nominations, Voter register and Polling stations at Nominations open; Results at Polling open; Leadership once declared). Edit election and Delete election (drafts only) are in the ⋯ menu. The year can change only while the election is a draft, and the register type only until the register is taken.

Positions

The Positions tab lists the posts to be filled, such as School President, Games Captain or Class President, with their races, seats, the grades that vote and the grades that may stand.

  1. Click Add position and type the Position name and the number of Seats.
  2. Under Races, choose how the post is contested:
    • One for the whole school: all the chosen grades vote in one race (School President).
    • One per school section: a separate race in each section, such as Upper primary and Junior School (Senator).
    • One per grade: a separate race in each grade (Grade Prefect).
    • One per class: a separate race in each class (Class Representative).
  3. Choose a Gender rule if needed: Candidates of one gender (pick the gender, and tick Only voters of that gender vote if only they vote, as for a Girls' Representative), or Seats for each gender (for example one boy and one girl per class).
  4. For a post elected with a deputy, tick Joint ticket and type the running mate's title (for example "Deputy President"). One vote elects both. A joint ticket has one seat.
  5. Under Grades that vote, tick the grades (click a section's button to tick all its grades). The box below shows the races this will make.
  6. If only some of those grades may stand, tick Only some of these grades may stand and tick them (for example only Grade 8 for President).
  7. Click Save position.

Nominations

Once nominations are open, the Nominations tab shows each position with its candidates. Click a position to open it. The counts at the top show how many are approved, how many await approval, and how many races still have no approved candidate.

  1. Click Nominate on the position (or on the race, when a position has several).
  2. Search for the student and click their name. Only students who may stand in that race are listed; one already standing elsewhere shows as taken.
  3. For a joint ticket, pick the running mate on the right.
  4. Type a Slogan if the candidate has one (it shows on the ballot), and click Nominate.

A new nomination waits as Nominated. On each candidate's card:

  • Approve puts the candidate on the ballot; Reject asks for a reason, which is shown on the card.
  • Unapprove sends an approved candidate back to waiting; Withdraw records that the candidate stood down; Restore brings back a rejected or withdrawn one.
  • The edit button changes the slogan or running mate; the trash button deletes a mistaken entry (reject or withdraw instead to keep a record).
  • The camera button uploads a photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB). The photo on the student's record is used if there is one. Photos can be changed until polling opens.

Use the Position, Status and Search filters to find candidates, and Print candidates for a list of the approved candidates. When nominations close, each approved candidate gets a ballot number (in name order).

  1. Find the student and click Issue ballot. Check the name, class and UPI shown are the student in front of you, then confirm.
  2. Hand the screen to the student. The ballot fills the screen: one section for each race they vote in, with each candidate's number, photo, class and slogan (and running mate). The heading says how many to choose, for example "Choose one".
  3. The student taps a candidate to choose them (tapping again removes the choice), then taps Review my vote.
  4. The student checks their choices. A race left empty shows as "No choice - left blank". Change goes back; Cast my vote records the vote.
  5. "Your vote has been recorded" appears. The student hands the screen back; click Next voter (it also returns by itself after a few seconds).

If a student cannot finish, the returning officer clicks Cancel on the ballot. Nothing is recorded and the student can be issued a ballot again.

Secret and final. A cast vote cannot be changed, and nobody can see how a student voted: ShuleNova records that the student voted separately from their choices. A student can vote only once, at their own station.
On a tablet or phone. The station screen is made for touch. While polling is open it keeps the screen awake where the browser allows it, and warns before a ballot in progress is lost to an accidental refresh. Use ↻ to refresh the counts.

When polling is not open the screen shows "Polling has not opened yet" or "Polling is closed" and no ballots can be issued.

Results

Elections > Results shows a card for each election that has opened polling. Click Live results (or Results once declared). Elections officers also find the count on the election's Results tab, marked Live while polling is open.

The results page shows the turnout, then a card for each race: every candidate's votes and percentage, with a crown on those leading (or elected). Below the races, the turnout at each station.

  • Until the results are declared they are marked Provisional and refresh by themselves (switch this off with Auto). Only elections officers see them before they are declared.
  • The Count checks tick means every race's ballots match the voters marked as voted. A race that does not match is flagged.
  • Pick one position from the list to show only its races. Slideshow shows the overview and then each race in turn, full screen, for a hall display; the full-screen button shows the page alone. Print or download the results as CSV with the buttons on the right.

Ties and declaring the results

After Close polling, the elections officer checks the count. A race where candidates tie for the last seat is marked Tied, and the results cannot be declared until it is settled:

  1. Draw lots in the presence of the candidates.
  2. Click Draw lots on the race, put the candidates in the order drawn with the arrows (the first takes the seat), and click Record the draw.

Then click Declare results. The leading candidates are declared elected and the results become final and visible to all staff.

Recording the winners

Once declared, the Leadership tab (or Leadership records on the results page) puts the winners in the students' records.

  1. For each position choose where its winners go: a Class role (for positions with a race per class; shown on the class) and/or a Responsibility for the year (shown on the report form). A running mate is recorded under the same responsibility with their title.
  2. Click Record winners. Each winner then shows Recorded with the date.

Communications

Communications sends SMS and email to parents and staff: notices you write yourself, and the school's standard messages (exam results, fee invoices, balance reminders and payment acknowledgements) sent from the pages where that information lives. Every message goes into a queue and is sent in the background, so you can carry on working while a large send goes out. The menu holds Communications > Send Message, Message Queue, Templates and SMS Report.

Messages cost money and cannot be called back. Each SMS is paid for by the part (see SMS length and cost). Always click Preview and read the message as the first recipient will get it before you click Send.

Sending needs one of the roles Administrator, HeadTeacher or Accounts (or SuperAdministrator), and access to the page you send from. If Send answers "You are not allowed to send messages", ask your administrator.

Send Message

Communications > Send Message writes a message of your own, such as a closing-day notice or a meeting reminder, and sends it to a group of parents or staff. The left side holds 1. Recipients and 2. Message; the right side shows the Preview.

Banners at the top. A yellow "Test mode is on" banner means messages are recorded but do not reach real recipients: your administrator is still setting things up. A red "SMS sending is switched off for this school" (or email) under the message means that channel cannot be used yet.

SMS length and cost

Under the SMS text the page counts the characters and the SMS parts each recipient will cost. The gateway charges per part:

  • Up to 160 characters is one part. A longer message is split into parts of 153 characters.
  • An emoji or a letter outside the SMS alphabet (most accented letters, for example) changes the whole message to another encoding: 70 characters in one part, 67 per part beyond. The page warns in red that this "more than doubles the cost". Curly quotes and long dashes are fine; they are sent as plain ones.
  • A few signs, such as [ ] { } ~ | €, count as two characters.
  • Placeholders are counted after they are filled in: a long name can push a message into a second part. The preview shows the real total.
Keep it to one part. A notice to 400 parents that runs to 161 characters costs 800 parts instead of 400. Shorten it until the counter says 1 part.

Previewing and sending

  1. Click Preview. The right side shows how many Recipients, SMS, SMS parts and Emails the send will make, the message as the first recipient gets it, and the list of everyone it goes to.
  2. Check the yellow note: recipients with no phone number or email for the chosen channel are skipped. Their phone or email shows in red in the list.
  3. Click Send. A box asks "Send message?" and states once more what will be queued. Click Send in it to confirm.
  4. ShuleNova says how many messages were queued and opens the Message Queue on your send, where you can watch it go out.

Clear empties the form and starts again. If something is missing, ShuleNova says what, for example "Choose at least one class." or "Type the SMS text."

Message Queue

Communications > Message Queue shows every message sent, from this page and from the "send to parents" buttons elsewhere. The page refreshes itself every 15 seconds while messages are still waiting; Refresh checks at once.

The Sends tab

Each send (all the messages queued at once) is one row: when and by whom it was queued, its title and recipients, its type, the number of messages, a progress bar with how many are still waiting, how many were delivered or failed, and the SMS parts used.

  • Click a send's title to see its messages on the Messages tab.
  • The blue ↻ button (Retry failed) queues the send's failed messages again.
  • The red ⊘ button (Cancel waiting messages) stops the messages not yet sent. ShuleNova asks first; messages already sent are not affected.

Every single message, newest first. The buttons along the top filter by status and show how many messages have each one. Retries

ShuleNova retries a message the gateway could not take by itself: up to 5 attempts, waiting 2, 4, 8 and then 16 minutes between them. If SMS (or email) is switched off or not set up, the messages simply wait and are checked again every 10 minutes; this does not use up attempts. Delivery reports are collected every few minutes.

Click a message to see all its details: the recipient, the number it went to, its status, when it was queued, sent and delivered, the attempts, the gateway's reply and any error, and the full text. In this window, Retry queues a failed or cancelled message again and Cancel stops one still queued. The same buttons appear at the end of the message's row.

Before you retry. Retrying an "Invalid mobile number" fails again. Correct the number on the parent's or learner's record first, then send that parent a new message.

Templates

Communications > Templates holds the wording ShuleNova uses:

  • Standard message types: exam results, new fee invoice, fee balance reminder, fee payment received and fee payment by a sponsor. Each has its channel (SMS, email or both), an Active switch and its wording. These are what the "send to parents" buttons send.
  • Saved templates for custom messages: wording you send often, offered in Start from a saved template on Send Message. Only active templates are offered.

Administrators set up and change the templates; see the administrator guide. The text in braces, such as {Balance}, is filled in for each learner when the message is sent.

SMS Report

Communications > SMS Report totals the SMS the school sent in a month, to check against the SMS provider's bill. Pick the Month and Year; the report opens on the current month.

  • SMS sent and SMS parts (billable): the messages the gateway accepted, and the parts you pay for.
  • Delivered, with how many were not delivered and how many still await a delivery report.
  • Amount: the parts times the price per part. It shows "-" until an administrator enters the price in the gateway settings.
  • By message type, By day and Every SMS break the month down.

Test messages and SMS that failed to send are not counted; the line under the cards says how many were left out. Months run in East Africa Time. Export CSV downloads every SMS of the month for a spreadsheet; Print prints the report.
