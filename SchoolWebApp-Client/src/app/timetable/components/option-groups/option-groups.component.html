Option groups

Subjects in a group are taught at the same time: learners split between them, each subject with its own teacher - e.g. CRE, IRE and HRE, or a Senior School elective block. The group meets as often as its busiest subject. If two subjects in a group have the same teacher for a class, they are timetabled one after the other instead.
