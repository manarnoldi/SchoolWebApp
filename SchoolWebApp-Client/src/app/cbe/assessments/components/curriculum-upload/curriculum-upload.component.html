1. Download the template
Subject missing? Add it to the grade's education level under Settings → Dropdowns → Academics → Edu-Level Subjects.

One workbook per subject and grade that holds the whole KICD curriculum design: a row per sub-strand with its specific learning outcomes, learning experiences, key inquiry questions, core competencies, values, PCIs and links to other learning areas; the assessment rubrics; the subject general learning outcomes; the essence statement and time allocation; the appendix lists; and every other section word for word. It comes pre-filled with anything already saved, so it can also be used to correct a subject. The Instructions sheet explains each column.

2. Upload the filled workbook
The workbook is checked first - nothing is saved until you confirm. Fixed something? Choose the file again.
