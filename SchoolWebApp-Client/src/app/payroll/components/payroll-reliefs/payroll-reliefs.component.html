How it is worked out
The relief is that percentage of what the employee paid towards this deduction. Optional. Linking a fixed relief to a deduction gives it only to employees who actually pay that deduction.
Given to everyone on the payroll, the way personal relief is. A percentage relief can only apply where the deduction it is based on exists, so this does not apply.
A relief comes off the tax itself once the bands have been applied. That is different from a tax deductible deduction, which comes off income before the bands - that is set on the deduction type.
