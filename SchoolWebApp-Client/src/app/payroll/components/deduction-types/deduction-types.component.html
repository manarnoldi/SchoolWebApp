Calculation
Payroll works this one out itself from the statutory rates, so it is not applied from the settings below.
Deducted from everyone on the payroll without adding it to each employee. An employee who has their own line keeps that amount.
Tax Treatment
Pooled with the employee's NSSF. The pool comes off taxable income at the lowest of the actual total, a percentage of basic pay, and a monthly cap - both limits are set under Payroll Settings > Rates & Limits.
Comes off gross pay before PAYE is worked out, capped on its own - a post-retirement medical fund, for instance. Not needed - a retirement contribution is already relieved through the NSSF pool above.
Leave blank for no cap.
A deduction that attracts a relief - insurance premiums, for instance - is named by the relief itself, under Payroll Settings > Reliefs.
Accounting
Money deducted from pay is owed onward - to the pension scheme, the SACCO, the union. Give each its own account to see what is owed to whom. Loan and advance recoveries go to Staff Loans & Advances automatically.
