Teaching Management

Teaching Management holds a teacher's professional documents for each subject and class they teach: the scheme of work for the term, the weekly lesson plans prepared from it, and the record of work kept after each lesson. Schemes and lesson plans go to the Head of Department for approval; the Tracker shows at a glance who has done what. The Curriculum page shows the KICD curriculum design that all of these draw on.

You find it under Teaching Management in the menu.

Who sees what. Teachers see and edit their own documents, for the subjects and classes they are allocated under Staff > Subject Allocation. A Head of Department (the head set on a department) also sees every document in their department, and approves and checks them. Administrators, and roles given Whole-school Access (Admin) in Menu Permissions, see the whole school. That menu item is a permission, not a page of its own: clicking it opens the Tracker.

Schemes of Work

A scheme of work lays out the term week by week and lesson by lesson: strand, sub-strand, lesson learning outcomes, learning experiences, key inquiry questions, learning resources and assessment methods. There is one scheme per subject, class and term. By default a scheme is due by the end of the seventh day after the term opens; the due date shows beside its status.

Finding your subjects

The page opens on your own subjects for the current term. Each row is a subject and class you teach, with the scheme's status and how many lessons it plans.

  • Change the Year and Term to see another term.
  • Heads of department and administrators have a Showing button that switches between My subjects and My department(s) (or Whole school). The Teacher and Department filters then appear.
  • Narrow the list with Grade, Subject, Scheme status or Search (teacher, subject or class). Clear removes the filters.
No subjects listed? "You have no subjects allocated for this term" means no allocation has been made for you yet. If the page says your account is not linked to a staff record, ask the administrator to link it under Security > Users.

Starting a scheme

  1. Find the subject and class and click Start scheme.
  2. Confirm with Start. The scheme is laid out over the term's weeks; you can change the number of weeks afterwards.
  3. The scheme opens. Fill it in in one of three ways:
    • Fill from curriculum lays the curriculum's sub-strands out across the weeks in order, giving each the number of lessons the curriculum sets, and skipping sub-strands already in an earlier term's scheme. Then check and edit each lesson.
    • Copy from… lists other schemes for the same subject and grade (another stream, or an earlier year). Click Copy on one, then adjust it for your class.
    • Type the lessons in yourself.
  4. Click Save. An asterisk (Save *) means there are unsaved changes.

Editing the lessons

At the top, enter the References (the books and guides the scheme follows), the number of Weeks and Lessons per week; after changing these, click Apply to the table. Weeks with lessons filled in cannot be cut off: clear or remove their lessons first.

The table is grouped by week. Click a week's bar to open or fold its lessons; the bar says how many lessons are filled in.

  • Pick the Strand and Sub-strand from the curriculum, or choose "- other (type) -" and type your own. The sub-strand list shows each one's lesson count and the weeks it is already scheduled in.
  • same as above copies the previous lesson's strand and sub-strand; refill replaces the lesson's text with the curriculum's wording.
  • + lesson on a week bar adds a lesson; the red × removes one (and anything recorded against it in the record of work).
  • The blue button at the end of a row opens the lesson in a larger form. Apply puts your changes into the scheme, and Previous / Next move between lessons; the scheme is only saved when you click Save on the page.
  • A week with no lessons (half-term, exams) is fine.

A scheme that has been submitted or approved is shown read-only, like the one above. Print prints it at any time.

Working in Excel

If you prefer Excel, click Download my workbook on the list. It holds every subject and class you teach this term, one sheet each. Fill it in and click Upload workbook. A single scheme also has its own Excel and Upload buttons while it is a draft.

The workbook is checked first and nothing is saved until you confirm. Sheets left empty are passed over; a sheet with errors is not saved until you fix it and upload again. A Head of Department can download a teacher's workbook (pick the teacher in the filter first) and upload it for them, for example when typing in paper schemes. Uploading saves the schemes as drafts; you still submit each one from its page.

Submitting for approval

  1. Save the scheme. The Approval box below the table lists the workflow's steps. By default there is one, Head of Department, and the head of the subject's department is already picked.
  2. Check the approver for each step (you cannot pick yourself), then click Submit for Approval. Unsaved changes are saved first.
  3. The scheme is now Submitted and locked. The approver sees it under the bell in the top bar.

If it comes back Returned (or Rejected), the approver's comment shows in the Approval box. Make the changes, save, and click Resubmit for Approval.

Approved too soon? Once approved, a scheme cannot be edited. A Super Administrator can use Reverse Approval to send it back to the teacher as a draft.

Approving Schemes and Lesson Plans

Schemes of work and each week's lesson plans follow the same approval steps, set up by the administrator under Settings > Approval Workflows. Out of the box there is one step, the Head of Department; a school can add more, such as the Deputy Head Teacher.

  1. Click the bell in the top bar and open the item waiting for you, or open the document from the Tracker.
  2. Read the scheme or the week's plans.
  3. In Your action, type a comment and click Approve, Return (back to the teacher to correct) or Reject. A comment is required to return or reject.

With several steps, approving passes the document to the next approver; it becomes Approved after the last step.

Lesson Plans

Lesson plans are prepared a week at a time from the scheme of work, and a week's plans are submitted and approved together. By default a week's plans are due at 08:00 on the first day of that week.

Preparing a week

  1. Open Teaching Management > Lesson Plans, find the subject and class, and click Lesson plans. (A subject shows "No scheme yet" until its scheme of work is started.)
  2. Pick a week in the strip at the top. Each button is coloured by status, and outlined in red when overdue. The current week opens by itself.
  3. For a week not prepared yet, click Prepare week N's lesson plans. A plan is made for each of the scheme's lessons that week, with the strand, sub-strand, outcomes, inquiry questions and resources filled in and a suggested date.

Completing each lesson

Click a lesson's bar to open it. Fill in or adjust:

  • Lesson number, Date, Start and End time, and the Roll (learners in the class).
  • Strand and Sub-strand, Specific learning outcomes, Learning resources, Key inquiry question(s) and Organisation of learning.
  • Lesson flow (click to unfold): Introduction, Lesson development as numbered steps (+ Step, move up and down, remove), Conclusion and Extended activities, with the minutes for each part.

Click Save lesson N, or Save all at the top for every changed lesson. A lesson can only be saved with its date, start and end time (end after start), roll and the minutes in the lesson flow; the page tells you what is missing. Each lesson's badge shows Unsaved changes, Incomplete or Ready.

  • Refresh from scheme pulls in changes made to the scheme since the week was prepared. Fill blanks keeps what you typed; Replace overwrites with the scheme's wording. Dates and times are kept.
  • Lesson adds an extra plan; Remove lesson removes one.
  • Print week prints the week's plans (save first: unsaved changes are not printed).

When the week is complete, submit it in the Approval box exactly as for a scheme. Submitted plans are locked unless returned.

Once a week is approved, each lesson shows an After the lesson panel with what the record of work says about it, and a link to record it there.

Record of Work

The record of work is kept after teaching: for every lesson in the scheme, whether it was taught, when, and what was covered. Only the teacher who owns the scheme fills it in.

  1. Open Teaching Management > Record of Work and click Record of work on the subject and class.
  2. Click a week's bar to open it. Each row is a lesson planned in the scheme.
  3. Set the Status (Taught, Partly taught or Not taught), the Date taught (required for taught and partly taught), the Work covered and any Remarks, such as "Not taught - sports day; carried to next week".
  4. Click Save. The button counts the lessons changed.

The bar at the top shows coverage: lessons taught, partly taught (counted as half) and not taught, out of those planned, and how many weeks have been checked.

Checking a teacher's record (Heads of Department)

Once a teacher has recorded at least one lesson in a week, the Head of Department (or whole-school staff) sees Check week N on the week's bar. Click it, add an optional comment for the teacher, and click Checked. A checked week is locked for the teacher. The person who checked it, or an administrator, can click remove check to let the teacher change it again. You cannot check your own record of work.

Tracker

The Tracker is one grid for the term: a row for each teacher, subject and class, with a cell for the scheme, one for each week's lesson plans, and the record of work. Teachers see their own subjects, Heads of Department their department(s) too, and administrators the whole school.

  • Cells are coloured by status and marked D (draft), S (submitted), R (returned), X (rejected) or ✓ (approved). A red outline means overdue. Hover over a cell to see its status and due date.
  • The row under the headings counts how many of each column are submitted or approved.
  • The Record of work column shows lessons taught out of those planned, and the number of weeks checked.
  • Click a status label above the grid (Draft, Submitted, Overdue and so on) to show only rows with a document in that state; click it again, or clear, to show all.
  • Filter by Teacher, Department, Grade, Subject or Search.
  • Click any cell to open that scheme, week of lesson plans or record of work; Back to tracker brings you back. The two buttons on the right export the grid to CSV and print it.
For heads of department. Click Submitted to see everything waiting for approval, and Overdue to see who to follow up.

Curriculum

The Curriculum page shows the full KICD curriculum design for a subject and grade, as loaded into ShuleNova. It is read-only; the administrator maintains it.

  1. Choose the Grade, then the Subject / learning area.
  2. The design opens with its essence statement and the subject's general learning outcomes, then the strands. Click a strand to open it.
  3. Print prints the design.

For each sub-strand you see its number of lessons, the specific learning outcomes, suggested learning experiences and key inquiry questions, followed by the core competencies, values, pertinent contemporary issues, links to other learning areas, suggested resources and assessment methods, and the assessment rubric (Exceeds, Meets, Approaches and Below Expectations).

This is the same curriculum that Fill from curriculum and the strand and sub-strand lists in schemes and lesson plans use. If a subject shows "has not been loaded yet", ask the administrator to load it.
