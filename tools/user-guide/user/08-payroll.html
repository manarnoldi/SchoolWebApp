Payroll

ShuleNova pays your staff every month: it works out each person's gross pay, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, the Housing Levy, other deductions and loan instalments, prints the payslips and produces the returns and bank schedule. The monthly work is done in this order:

  1. Employee Salaries: who is paid, and how much.
  2. Loans & Advances: any loan or advance to recover from pay.
  3. Processing: create the month, process it, check the payslips, and send it for approval.
  4. Reports: once it is approved, print the payslips, muster roll, statutory returns, bank schedule and P9 cards.

All four are under Payroll in the menu. The rates and rules behind the calculation (tax bands, NSSF bands, reliefs and so on) are set up once by an administrator; see the administrator guide.

Who sees payroll. Payroll pages are for the people your school has made payroll officers. Every other member of staff sees only their own payslips, under My Payslips.

Employee Salaries

Payroll > Employee Salaries lists every active member of staff, one row each, with their basic salary, house, transport and other allowances, gross pay, the date the salary takes effect and whether it is active. The badges at the top count the staff with a salary, without one, and those excluded from payroll.

Setting or changing salaries

  1. Type the amounts straight into the row: Basic Salary, House Allow., Transport Allow. and Other Allow. The Gross column adds them up as you type, and a changed row turns yellow.
  2. Give each changed row an Effective Date. To use the same date for everyone listed, pick it in the Effective Date box at the top and click Apply to all.
  3. Click Save All. The number on the button shows how many rows will be saved. Discard throws away the changes you have not saved.

Use the Staff Member and Show filters (all, with salary, without salary, excluded from payroll) to narrow the list. A member of staff marked Not set has no salary yet and will not be paid until they have one.

Additional earnings and voluntary deductions

Some staff get more than the four standard amounts (overtime, a responsibility allowance) or have something taken off besides the statutory deductions (welfare, SACCO, union dues, an insurance premium). These are kept per employee:

  1. Click the list button (☰) at the end of the row. The Edit Employee Salary window opens.
  2. Under Additional Earnings click Add Earning, choose the Earning Type and type the amount. Do the same under Voluntary Deductions with Add Deduction.
  3. Check the Gross Total, Total Deductions and Net Salary at the bottom, then click Save.

The net salary shown here leaves out PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy; those are worked out when the payroll is processed. The number badge beside a name in the grid shows how many extra lines that person has.

A new employee. For someone with no salary yet, the window shows only the salary details. Save them first; the window then reopens with the earnings and deductions sections.

Statutory numbers and staff not on the payroll

A yellow warning above the grid counts the staff missing a KRA PIN, ID, NSSF or SHA number. Fix these before you process the payroll, or those lines print blank on the payslips and returns. Click the card button at the start of the row's actions (it shows a warning sign while something is missing) to open Payroll Details:

  • Fill in the KRA Pin, ID Number, NSSF No and SHA No and click Save. They are saved on the staff record.
  • Switch on Exclude from payroll for people on the staff list who are not paid through payroll, such as suppliers or contractors. They stay on the staff list but are skipped when the payroll is processed and get no payslip.

Names, staff category and employment details are changed on the staff record (Staff > Staff Details), not here.

Who gets paid. Processing pays every active member of staff who has an active salary and is not excluded. Someone whose employment date is after the end of the month is not paid for that month. The trash button deletes a salary record; the person stays on the list but will not be paid.

Loans & Advances

Payroll > Loans & Advances lists the loans and salary advances being recovered from pay, with the principal, the monthly deduction, the balance still owed and the status (Active, Fully Paid or Cancelled).

Recording a loan or advance

  1. Click Add Loan / Advance.
  2. Choose the Staff Member and the Issue Date, and leave the Status at Active.
  3. Type the Principal Amount and the Monthly Deduction (both must be more than 0) and the Balance still to recover, which for a new loan is normally the principal.
  4. Add a Description (for example "Salary advance") and any Notes, then click Save.

Each time the payroll is processed, every active loan takes its monthly deduction (or the balance, if that is smaller) from the employee's pay, and the balance goes down. When the balance reaches zero the loan becomes Fully Paid by itself. If a month is processed again, its instalments are put back first, so nothing is taken twice.

Stopping a loan. To stop recovering a loan, edit it and set the status to Cancelled. Use the filters at the top to see one person's loans, or only active ones.

Processing

Payroll > Processing lists the payroll months (periods) with their status, the number of payslips and the totals of gross pay, net pay and PAYE. Click any row to see its payslips.

StatusWhat it means
DraftThe month exists but has not been processed. It can be processed or deleted.
Processed (Not Approved)Payslips have been worked out. Nothing is final yet: the month can be processed again after a correction.
Awaiting review / Awaiting final approvalSent for approval and with the approvers. It cannot be processed again until an approver returns it.
Returned for changes / RejectedThe approvers sent it back. Make the correction, process it again, and resubmit.
ApprovedLocked. Its payslips and reports can be printed, staff can see their payslips, and its journal goes to the accounts.

Creating the month

  1. Click New Period.
  2. Choose the Month, check the Year, and click Save. The period is named after the month (for example "November 2026"). There can be only one period for each month.

Processing the payroll

  1. Make sure this month's salary changes, extra earnings and deductions, and loans are all entered.
  2. On the Draft period's row, click the cog button (Process) and confirm.
  3. ShuleNova works out every payslip and the period becomes Processed (Not Approved).

Found a mistake? Fix the salary or loan, then click the re-process button (↻) on the period. Re-processing throws the period's payslips away and builds them again from the current salaries.

NSSF bands. If processing stops with "No NSSF bands are in force", the NSSF rates for that month have not been set up. Ask your administrator.

Checking the payslips

Before sending the payroll for approval, check it. Click the period (or its eye button) to open its payslips: each employee's gross pay, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, PAYE, total deductions and net pay, with the totals at the bottom. A yellow Missing ... badge beside a name shows which statutory numbers that person lacks.

Click the eye button on a row to see that payslip exactly as it will print, and use Previous and Next to go through them one by one. Back to the list returns to the table.

Approval

Most schools have the payroll checked by a reviewer and approved by a final approver. Click the approval button (person with a tick) on a processed period:

  1. The window shows the payslip count, gross pay, PAYE and net pay.
  2. Pick the approver for each step and click Submit for Approval. The period now reads Awaiting review.
  3. Each approver finds it under the bell (and as Your turn on the period). They open the approval window, type a comment and click Approve, Return (send back for changes) or Reject. A comment is required to return or reject.
  4. When the final approver approves, the period becomes Approved and is locked.

If your school has switched the payroll approval off, a single Approve button (✓) appears on the processed period instead.

After approval. An approved payroll cannot be processed again or deleted. Approving also creates the salary journal in the accounts, dated the last day of the month. Only a Draft period can be deleted.

Reports

Payroll > Reports covers approved payrolls only; a payroll still being checked does not appear. Choose the Payroll Period (the latest approved month opens first) and a tab. Each tab has its own Print button.

TabWhat it shows
Muster RollEvery employee's pay and deductions in one table, with totals.
Statutory ReturnsThe PAYE, NSSF (employee and employer), SHIF and Housing Levy (employee and employer) to remit, and any levy the school pays itself (such as NITA).
Bank ScheduleEach employee's bank, branch, account number and net pay, for the bank transfer.
PayslipsThe payslips: print one, or Print All, two to an A4 sheet. Staff excluded from payroll are skipped.
P9 Tax CardsThe KRA P9A card for a year: pick the year and the employee, then Print one card or Print all.
Printing payslips straight after approval. In the payslips window on the Processing page, an approved period shows a Print payslips link that opens this page on the Payslips tab for that month.

My Payslips

Every member of staff paid through ShuleNova can see and print their own payslips. Click your name in the account menu to open My Profile, then click My payslips.

  • Each month's gross pay, deductions and net pay is listed, newest first. Click Payslip to print that month's slip.
  • A payslip appears once that month's payroll is approved.
  • Your P9 tax card for a year appears under P9 tax cards once the year has ended and all its payroll is approved. Print it for your tax return.

If the page says your sign-in is not linked to a staff record, ask your administrator to link your user account to your staff record.
