Communications

Communications sends SMS and email to parents and staff: notices you write yourself, and the school's standard messages (exam results, fee invoices, balance reminders and payment acknowledgements) sent from the pages where that information lives. Every message goes into a queue and is sent in the background, so you can carry on working while a large send goes out. The menu holds Communications > Send Message, Message Queue, Templates and SMS Report.

Messages cost money and cannot be called back. Each SMS is paid for by the part (see SMS length and cost). Always click Preview and read the message as the first recipient will get it before you click Send.

Sending needs one of the roles Administrator, HeadTeacher or Accounts (or SuperAdministrator), and access to the page you send from. If Send answers "You are not allowed to send messages", ask your administrator.

Send Message

Communications > Send Message writes a message of your own, such as a closing-day notice or a meeting reminder, and sends it to a group of parents or staff. The left side holds 1. Recipients and 2. Message; the right side shows the Preview.

Banners at the top. A yellow "Test mode is on" banner means messages are recorded but do not reach real recipients: your administrator is still setting things up. A red "SMS sending is switched off for this school" (or email) under the message means that channel cannot be used yet.

Writing the message

  1. In Send by, choose SMS, Email or SMS & Email. The page shows the boxes that channel needs: SMS text, and Email subject and Email body.
  2. To reuse saved wording, pick it in Start from a saved template. It fills in the channel and the text, which you can still change.
  3. Type a Title, such as "Half-term break". It is not sent; it names the send in the Message Queue so you can find it later.
  4. Type the message. To put each recipient's own details into it, click in the text where they should go and click a button beside Insert:, for example {ParentName} or {StudentName}. Hover over a button to see what it stands for.

SMS length and cost

Under the SMS text the page counts the characters and the SMS parts each recipient will cost. The gateway charges per part:

  • Up to 160 characters is one part. A longer message is split into parts of 153 characters.
  • An emoji or a letter outside the SMS alphabet (most accented letters, for example) changes the whole message to another encoding: 70 characters in one part, 67 per part beyond. The page warns in red that this "more than doubles the cost". Curly quotes and long dashes are fine; they are sent as plain ones.
  • A few signs, such as [ ] { } ~ | €, count as two characters.
  • Placeholders are counted after they are filled in: a long name can push a message into a second part. The preview shows the real total.
Keep it to one part. A notice to 400 parents that runs to 161 characters costs 800 parts instead of 400. Shorten it until the counter says 1 part.

Previewing and sending

  1. Click Preview. The right side shows how many Recipients, SMS, SMS parts and Emails the send will make, the message as the first recipient gets it, and the list of everyone it goes to.
  2. Check the yellow note: recipients with no phone number or email for the chosen channel are skipped. Their phone or email shows in red in the list.
  3. Click Send. A box asks "Send message?" and states once more what will be queued. Click Send in it to confirm.
  4. ShuleNova says how many messages were queued and opens the Message Queue on your send, where you can watch it go out.

Clear empties the form and starts again. If something is missing, ShuleNova says what, for example "Choose at least one class." or "Type the SMS text."

Message Queue

Communications > Message Queue shows every message sent, from this page and from the "send to parents" buttons elsewhere. The page refreshes itself every 15 seconds while messages are still waiting; Refresh checks at once.

The Sends tab

Each send (all the messages queued at once) is one row: when and by whom it was queued, its title and recipients, its type, the number of messages, a progress bar with how many are still waiting, how many were delivered or failed, and the SMS parts used.

  • Click a send's title to see its messages on the Messages tab.
  • The blue ↻ button (Retry failed) queues the send's failed messages again.
  • The red ⊘ button (Cancel waiting messages) stops the messages not yet sent. ShuleNova asks first; messages already sent are not affected.

The Messages tab

Every single message, newest first. The buttons along the top filter by status and show how many messages have each one. Below them, filter by Channel, Type and dates From and To, or type a name or number in Search and press Enter. Clear removes the filters. When you came from one send, "Showing one send only" appears with a link to Show all messages.

Retries

ShuleNova retries a message the gateway could not take by itself: up to 5 attempts, waiting 2, 4, 8 and then 16 minutes between them. If SMS (or email) is switched off or not set up, the messages simply wait and are checked again every 10 minutes; this does not use up attempts. Delivery reports are collected every few minutes.

Click a message to see all its details: the recipient, the number it went to, its status, when it was queued, sent and delivered, the attempts, the gateway's reply and any error, and the full text. In this window, Retry queues a failed or cancelled message again and Cancel stops one still queued. The same buttons appear at the end of the message's row.

Before you retry. Retrying an "Invalid mobile number" fails again. Correct the number on the parent's or learner's record first, then send that parent a new message.

Templates

Communications > Templates holds the wording ShuleNova uses:

  • Standard message types: exam results, new fee invoice, fee balance reminder, fee payment received and fee payment by a sponsor. Each has its channel (SMS, email or both), an Active switch and its wording. These are what the "send to parents" buttons send.
  • Saved templates for custom messages: wording you send often, offered in Start from a saved template on Send Message. Only active templates are offered.

Administrators set up and change the templates; see the administrator guide. The text in braces, such as {Balance}, is filled in for each learner when the message is sent.

SMS Report

Communications > SMS Report totals the SMS the school sent in a month, to check against the SMS provider's bill. Pick the Month and Year; the report opens on the current month.

  • SMS sent and SMS parts (billable): the messages the gateway accepted, and the parts you pay for.
  • Delivered, with how many were not delivered and how many still await a delivery report.
  • Amount: the parts times the price per part. It shows "-" until an administrator enters the price in the gateway settings.
  • By message type, By day and Every SMS break the month down.

Test messages and SMS that failed to send are not counted; the line under the cards says how many were left out. Months run in East Africa Time. Export CSV downloads every SMS of the month for a spreadsheet; Print prints the report.

Except for the release tick, each button first shows a "Send to parents?" box: how many SMS (and parts) and emails will go, how many learners have no parent contact and will be skipped, and the first message exactly as it will read. Click Send to queue them, or Cancel. When they are queued, click the message that appears to open the Message Queue on that send.

If nothing is sent. "The 'Fee payment received' message type is switched off" means an administrator has not turned that message type on under Templates (payment acknowledgements start switched off). "Nobody to send to" means none of the parents has a phone number or email for the message type's channel.

The send buttons appear only when your school has the Communications module.
