Staff

The Staff menu holds the school's teaching and non-teaching staff: their details and employment records, their daily attendance, and which teacher takes which subject in each class.

PageWhat it is for
Staff DetailsThe list of staff, each staff member's details, and their full record.
AttendanceThe daily staff register.
Subject AllocationChoosing the teacher of each subject in a class.
ReportsStaff attendance, month by month and day by day, and who teaches what.

Staff Details

Staff > Staff Details lists the staff. It opens on all Active staff. To narrow it, choose a Staff category (teaching or non-teaching), an Employment type (such as permanent, contract or casual) or another status, and click Search. Type in the Search box above the list to find someone by name or number.

As in the learners list, the Action icons view (eye), edit (pencil) and delete (bin) a staff member, and the spanner opens their full record. Suspended staff show in yellow and terminated staff in red.

A staff member's details

The eye icon shows their details: name, nationality, religion, gender and status on the left, and three tabs on the right:

  • Personal details: employment/TSC number, ID, staff number, date of birth, SHA, NSSF and KRA PIN numbers, and bank details.
  • Employment details: employment date and end date, employment type, staff category and designation (Teacher, Deputy Head Teacher, Accountant and so on).
  • Contact details: phone, email, address and other details.

Click Edit staff details at the top right to change them, then save. Keep the statutory and bank numbers correct: payroll uses them on payslips and returns.

Adding a staff member

  1. Click Add staff at the top right of the list.
  2. Fill in at least the fields marked *: Full name, Nationality, Religion, Gender, the Employment/TSC number, and on the Employment details tab the Employment type, Staff category and Designation.
  3. Save the record.
Teaching staff. Only staff in the Teaching category can be given subjects and classes, and appear in the teacher lists. A staff member's sign-in to ShuleNova is a separate user account, set up by an administrator.
Someone who leaves. Don't delete a staff member who leaves: edit them and set Staff status to Terminated. Their records are kept and they drop off the active lists.

A staff member's full record

The spanner opens a summary of the staff member with these tabs below it:

Tip. The Subjects tab, like other year filters, may open on the latest year set up, which can be next year. Pick the current year and click Search.

Staff Attendance

Staff > Attendance is the daily staff register, usually kept by the deputy head or the secretary.

  1. Choose the date (no later than today). Leave Staff category and Employment type empty for everyone, or choose one to list only those staff. Click Search. The page opens on the last day a register was taken.
  2. Tick Present? for each staff member at work (the tick in the heading ticks everyone), and leave absent staff unticked.
  3. Set the Time In (24-hour clock), and type a reason under Remarks for anyone absent, such as "Official duty" or "Sick leave". Your school may require a remark for every absence.
  4. Click Update Staff Attendances and confirm.

A day already marked is shown as it was saved, so you can correct it and save again. The bin removes one staff member's record for the day.

Subject Allocation (Staff)

Staff > Subject Allocation sets the teacher of every subject in a class, on one screen. The teacher you set here is the one who enters that subject's marks and assessments for the class and prepares its teaching documents, so set it at the start of each year.

  1. Choose the Academic Year and the Class. The class's subjects are listed, each with its teacher.
  2. Pick a teacher in the Assigned Teacher box beside each subject, or -- Not Assigned -- to clear it. Rows you change turn yellow and show Modified, and a badge counts the unsaved changes.
  3. Click Save All Changes.
StatusMeaning
AllocatedThe subject has a teacher, saved.
UnassignedNo teacher yet. Optional subjects that no learner in the class takes can stay unassigned.
ModifiedChanged here but not yet saved.

A row in red is a subject allocated to the class that does not belong to its level; choose -- Remove allocation -- and save to clear it.

Staff Reports

Staff > Reports has the staff reports. Like the school reports, each opens already filled in; change the filters and click Search to see something else. A report opens on the first staff category that has staff, which may be Non-teaching: choose Teaching for the teachers. Printing opens a PDF in a new tab.

Attendance Report

The month's register for a staff category: a row per staff member and a column per day, P for present and A for absent. Choose the category, status, month and year, and click Search. Print prints the whole list; the printer icon at the end of a row prints one person's month.

Attendance Detailed

One staff member's attendance day by day, with the remarks given for absences. Choose the category, status, month and year and click Search, then click a name on the left. To print, tick the staff you want and click Print (Preview for one).

Subject Allocation Report

What each teacher teaches in a year. Choose the year, the staff category (Teaching) and status, and click Search. The number beside each name is how many subject-classes they teach, which shows at a glance who carries the heaviest load and who has nothing yet. Click a name to list their subjects and classes; tick names and click Print for a printed copy.
