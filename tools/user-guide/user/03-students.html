Students

The Students menu holds everything about your learners: their personal details, parents and guardians, the class they are in each year, their subjects and their daily attendance.

PageWhat it is for
Basic DetailsThe list of learners, each learner's details, and their full record (class, parents, discipline, attendance, results, fees).
ParentsParents and guardians, and which learners they are linked to.
Subject AllocationWhich subjects each learner in a class takes.
AttendanceTaking the daily register for a class.
Class AssignmentMoving learners into another class: promotion at the end of the year, or a correction during it.

Basic Details: the Learners List

Students > Basic Details lists the school's learners. It opens on the Active learners; to see others, choose Suspended, Terminated or Alumni in the status box and click Search.

Finding a learner

  1. Type part of the learner's name or admission number in the Search box above the list. The list narrows as you type.
  2. Click a column heading (for example Full name or Adm#) to sort by it; click again to reverse the order.
  3. Use entries per page and the page numbers at the bottom to see more.

The four icons in the Action column are:

IconWhat it does
EyeViews the learner's personal details.
PencilOpens the same details for changing.
BinDeletes the learner, after you confirm. Prefer changing their status (see below).
SpannerOpens the learner's full record, with tabs for class, parents, attendance, results and more.

Administrators also see Export to Excel, which saves the learners of the status chosen as a spreadsheet file.

A learner's details

The eye icon shows the learner's personal details: name, admission number, nationality, religion, gender, date of birth, contacts, learning mode (day or boarding), application and admission dates, address, health concerns and status. Everything is greyed out until you click Edit student details at the top right; Update record then saves your changes. Manage other student details opens the full record.

Adding a new learner

  1. Click Add student at the top right of the list.
  2. Fill in at least the fields marked *: Full name, Admission number, Nationality, Religion, Gender and Learning mode. Add a photo with Select student image if you have one.
  3. Click Save record and confirm.
  4. Then open the learner's full record (the spanner) to put them in a class and link their parents, as described next. A learner who is not in a class does not appear on class lists, registers or exams.
A learner who leaves. Don't delete a learner who has left or finished: edit their details and set Student status to Terminated (left the school), Alumni (finished) or Suspended. Their history (results, fees, attendance) is kept, and they drop off the Active lists. In the list, suspended learners show in yellow, terminated in red and alumni in green.

A Learner's Full Record

The spanner icon opens the learner's full record. A summary of their details sits at the top, and tabs below hold the rest. The tab you open is kept in the page address, so a refresh returns to it.

TabWhat it holds
Former school/sSchools the learner attended before: name, curriculum, level, class and last exam score and position.
Class detailsThe class the learner is in for each year. Pick a year and click Search; Add class puts the learner in a class.
Parents detailsThe learner's parents and guardians, with their relationship and whether they receive messages, pay fees or may pick the learner up.
Discipline recordsIncidents: dates, details, the type of occurrence and the outcome. Add discipline records one.
Subjects detailsThe subjects the learner takes (see Subject Allocation below).
Attendance detailsThe learner's attendance records. Add attendance records a single day for this learner.

The Academics tab appears only to staff allowed to see results.

The Fees tab appears only to staff allowed to see fees.

Seeing or changing a learner's class

  1. Open the Class details tab.
  2. Pick the year and click Search. The tab may open on next year; choose the current one.
  3. To put the learner in a class, click Add class, choose the year and class and save. To correct a class, use the pencil on its row.

For moving a whole class at once, use Class Assignment instead.

Linking a parent

  1. Open the Parents details tab.
  2. If the parent is already in ShuleNova (for example a brother or sister is already at the school), click Add existing parent, choose them and their relationship, and save.
  3. Otherwise click Add new parent, fill in the parent's details and their Relationship with student, and save. The new parent is linked to this learner.

Following a learner's results

The Academics tab shows the learner's progress through the year: their mean in each exam against the class mean (green at or above it, red below), or subject by subject with By subject. Below the chart, click an exam to open its marks. Change the Year, or narrow the list to one Term; Print gives report forms and the progress report.

Parents

Students > Parents lists all parents and guardians. Search and sort it the same way as the learners list.

Three ticks describe each parent:

TickMeaning
NotifiableReceives the school's messages about the learner (SMS and email from Communications).
PayerPays the learner's fees.
PickerMay collect the learner from school.

Viewing a parent and their children

  1. Click the eye icon on the parent's row.
  2. The left side shows their details. Open Parent's students on the right to see every learner linked to them; click a learner's name to open their record.

Adding or changing a parent

Click Add parent, or the pencil on a parent's row. The fields marked * are needed: Parent number, Full name, Nationality, Religion, Gender and Occupation. Set the Notifiable?, Payer? and Picker? ticks, add the phone number and email under Other information, and click Save record.

Tip. To add a parent and link them to a learner in one go, start from the learner's record (Parents details tab, Add new parent) rather than from this list.

Subject Allocation

Students > Subject Allocation sets which subjects each learner takes. Exams and assessments only have a place for a learner's mark in the subjects allocated to them, so allocate subjects at the start of the year, before the first exam.

Seeing a class's subjects

  1. Pick the curriculum, year, level and class at the top. The class loads as soon as it is chosen.
  2. Each learner's row lists their subjects.

Allocating subjects

  1. With the class shown, click Add allocations.
  2. Tick the learners on the left (Select all for the whole class) and the subjects on the right.
  3. Click Save. Every ticked learner gets every ticked subject; subjects a learner already has are skipped.
Religious education and options. Give the whole class its common subjects in one go with Select all, then add each option (such as CRE or IRE) to just the learners who take it.

Removing subjects

Click the × on a subject to remove it from one learner, or tick learners and click Delete selected to remove all of their subjects. ShuleNova asks you to confirm first.

Removing a subject removes its marks. If the learner already has exam results in that subject, they are deleted with it. The confirmation tells you how many results will go; read it before you click Delete.

Attendance

Students > Attendance is the class register. Class teachers mark it each day; the attendance reports under School > Reports are built from it.

Taking the register

  1. Pick the curriculum, year, level and class, and the date (no later than today), then click Search. The page opens on the first class and the last day a register was taken.
  2. The class's learners are listed. On a new day nobody is ticked yet: tick Present? in the heading to tick everyone, then untick the learners who are absent. A learner left unticked is saved as absent.
  3. Type a reason in Remarks for an absent learner (for example "Sick - parent called"). Your school may require a remark for every absence; ShuleNova then won't save until each absent learner has one.
  4. Change Time In if a learner came late.
  5. Click Update Student Attendances at the bottom and confirm.

Searching a day that already has a register shows it as it was saved, so you can correct it and save again. The bin on a row deletes that learner's record for the day.

Tip. The class, date and other choices stay in the page address. Bookmark the page with your class chosen and it opens straight on your class each morning.

Class Assignment (Promotion)

Students > Class Assignment moves learners from one class to another, many at a time. Use it at the end of the year to promote a class into next year's class, or during the year to correct a learner's class or stream (for example from 5E to 5W).

Before promoting. Next year's academic year and classes must already be set up by an administrator.

Promoting a class to next year

  1. Under From (Current Class), choose this year and the class. Under To (New Class), choose next year and the class the learners go up to. ShuleNova suggests the next grade in the same stream.
  2. Leave Also remove from source class unticked, so the learners keep their record in this year's class.
  3. Click Load Students. The learners still to move are listed under Students to Assign.
  4. Tick the learners to promote (the box in the heading ticks them all). Leave unticked any learner repeating the year or leaving.
  5. Click Assign Selected and confirm.

Learners already in the new class are listed under Already Assigned, so you can promote a class in several sittings without doubling anyone.

Correcting a class during the year

  1. Choose the same year on both sides, the learner's current class under From and the right class under To.
  2. Also remove from source class ticks itself when both years are the same; leave it ticked.
  3. Click Load Students, tick the learners, and click Move Selected.

A move keeps the learner's subjects, attendance and results for the year: they follow the learner into the new class.

Undoing a promotion

Tick learners under Already Assigned and click Reverse Selected. This removes them from the new class; their record in the old class stays. Reverse only before anything (subjects, attendance or marks) has been recorded for them in the new class.
