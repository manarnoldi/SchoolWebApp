Finance

The Finance menu is where the bursar and accounts clerks keep the school's money in order: the budget for the year, fee structures and the invoices raised from them, fee payments and receipts, sponsors who pay fees for learners, expenses, journal entries, and the reports and statements that come out of them. Behind every page is one set of books: an invoice, a payment or an approved expense goes straight into the ledger, so the reports are always up to date.

Before you start. Your administrator sets up the chart of accounts, the fee and expense categories, the finance settings and the approval workflows (see the Administrator Guide, Finance Set-up). If a list you need is empty, such as the fee categories when you build a fee structure, ask them.

How Approvals Work

Expenses, journal entries, budgets, budget amendments and credit or debit notes are not posted the moment they are saved. They go to one or more approvers first, in the steps your administrator set up under Settings > Approval Workflows. The form for each of these has an Approval box at the bottom.

  1. Fill in the form. In the Approval box, pick an approver for each step from the people who hold that step's role.
  2. Click Save & Submit for Approval. The record is sent to the first step's approver, who sees it under the bell at the top of the page. If you only click Save (no approvers picked), it stays a draft; open it again with the edit button and submit it when you are ready.
  3. The approver opens the record (the ✓✓ Review / Approve button in the list, or the link under the bell), reads it, writes a comment if they wish and clicks Approve, Return or Reject. A comment is required to return or reject.
  4. When the last step approves, the record takes effect: an expense or journal is posted to the ledger, an amendment changes the budget, a note changes the learner's invoice.
  • Returned records come back to you with the approver's comment shown at the top of the Approval box. Correct the record and click Resubmit for Approval.
  • Rejected records stop there. The comment says why.
  • Once submitted, a record is locked: the edit and delete buttons disappear. Depending on the school's setting, it stays editable until the first step approves. An approver can never edit or delete what they approve.
  • If the school switched on maker-checker for a workflow, you cannot pick yourself as an approver.
  • Only a Super Administrator can reverse an approval. The record then goes back to draft.
"No active workflow configured". If the Approval box shows this message, nobody can approve the record and it cannot be posted. Ask your administrator to add a workflow for that form.

Budget Register

A budget plan is the frame for one year's budgets: its name, academic year and dates. Each department then prepares its own budget inside the plan. Open Finance > Budgets > Budget Register.

Adding a budget plan

  1. Click Add Budget Plan.
  2. Give it a Name (for example "2027 Annual Budget"), choose the Academic Year, and check the Start Date and End Date (they start as 1 January to 31 December). The Code is given automatically.
  3. Set the Status: Draft while you prepare it, Open when departments may add budgets to it, Closed when the year is over.
  4. Click Save.

The number in the Budgets column opens the Budgets page showing that plan's budgets. A plan that already holds budgets cannot be deleted.

Only Open plans take budgets. If the Budgets form says "No Open budget plans found", set the plan's status to Open here first.

Budgets

A budget is one department's income and spending plan for a budget plan: so much expected from each income account, so much allowed for each expense account. Open Finance > Budgets > Budgets. The list opens on the current year's Open plan; use the Budget Plan, Department and Status filters to change it.

Preparing a department's budget

  1. Click Add Budget.
  2. Choose the Budget Plan. The academic year and dates fill in from the plan.
  3. Choose the Department. The Name fills in by itself; you may change it. Add a Description if you wish.
  4. Click Save & Add Lines. The form stays open and the budget lines appear.
  1. Under Income Budget Lines, click Add Income Line for each expected income: pick the income account and type the Budgeted Amount. Do the same under Expenditure Budget Lines with Add Expense Line.
  2. Check the summary at the bottom: total income, total expenditure and the net.
  3. Pick the approvers in the Approval box and click Save & Submit for Approval, or Save Changes to finish later.

A budget must have at least one line before it can be saved again. Once approved, a budget can no longer be edited; change it with a budget amendment.

Budget against actual

Click the chart (View vs Actual) button on a budget to see, for each line, the budgeted amount, any approved amendments, the effective budget, what has actually been received or spent in the ledger, and the variance. The approval history is at the bottom.

Budget Amendments

When a department needs more (or less) than it was given, raise a budget amendment rather than editing the budget. Open Finance > Budgets > Budget Amendments; filter by year, budget and status (Pending, Approved, Rejected).

Raising an amendment

  1. Click New Amendment and choose the Budget. Its current lines and effective amounts appear.
  2. Check the Amendment Date and type the Reason (required).
  3. Click Add Income Line or Add Expense Line, pick the account and type the New Amount. A higher amount increases the budget; a lower one reduces it. The Delta column shows the change.
  4. Check the Net Effect of this Amendment box: current income and expenditure, the change, and the projected net.
  5. Pick the approvers and click Save & Submit for Approval, or Save to submit later with the edit button.

The budget changes only when the amendment is fully approved; until then its status is Pending. The eye button shows an amendment's lines and approval history.

Fee Structures

A fee structure lists what learners at one learning level pay in one term: tuition, boarding, lunch, activity and so on, each a fee category with an amount. Invoices are generated from it. Open Finance > Fees > Fee Structures and filter by year, session (term), education level, learning level and status.

Adding fee structures

  1. Click Add Fee Structure.
  2. Choose the Academic Year and the Session (term), or tick All to make one for every term of the year.
  3. Choose the Education Level and the Learning Level (for example Grade 4), or tick All levels to make one for every level in that education level. The form tells you how many fee structures it will create.
  4. Click Add Item for each fee: choose the Fee Category, type the Amount, and tick Mandatory for fees every learner pays. Leave optional fees (transport, a club) unticked.
  5. Click Save. Each structure is named by itself from the year, term and learning level.

A fee category can appear only once in a structure. Click the number in the Items column to see a structure's fees.

Same fees for several levels? Tick All levels and All sessions to create the whole year in one go, then edit the few levels that differ.

Student Invoices

An invoice is what one learner owes for a term. Open Finance > Fees > Student Invoices. Filter by year, session, class, status (Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid, Overdue, Cancelled), sponsor, or search for a learner by name or admission number. Each row shows the total, what has been paid and the balance; a sponsor badge marks invoices a sponsor contributes to.

Invoicing a class for the term

  1. Click Bulk Generate.
  2. Choose the Academic Year, the Session, the Fee Structure and the Class.
  3. Set the Invoice Date, and the Due Date and Description if you want them.
  4. Click Generate Invoices and confirm. One invoice is made for every active learner in the class, with the structure's mandatory fees. A summary then shows how many learners had a sponsorship applied and how much sponsors have pledged.

Adding optional fees, discounts and sponsors to one invoice

  1. Click the edit (Edit items) button on the learner's invoice.
  2. Fees from the fee structure are marked Mandatory or Optional. Tick Include for the optional fees this learner takes. Mandatory fees cannot be unticked, and their amounts are locked: to change one, change the fee structure.
  3. Type a Discount on any line if the school has agreed one.
  4. To have a sponsor pay part of a line, choose the Sponsorship; the Sponsor pledge fills in from the sponsorship's cover and can be adjusted.
  5. For a one-off charge (a lost book, a trip), click Add Item, choose the fee category and type the amount. These Ad-hoc lines can be removed again.
  6. Click Save.
Sponsor pledges. The learner is still invoiced the whole fee. The pledge is the part the sponsor is expected to pay; if the sponsor does not pay it, the family owes it.

Click the number in the Items column to see an invoice in full: each fee, discount, sponsor pledge, what the sponsor and the family have paid, and the balance.

Sending invoices to parents

The paper plane button on a row sends that invoice to the learner's parents; Send to parents above the list sends every invoice listed, so filter to the class or term first. ShuleNova first shows how many SMS or emails will go out, which learners have no parent contact, and how the first message reads; click Send to queue them. The wording comes from the invoice template under Communications > Templates.

Fee Payments and Receipts

Every fee payment is recorded here and gets a receipt number. Open Finance > Fees > Fee Payments. Filter by year, session, class, learner, Paid by (the family, sponsors, or one sponsor), method and dates; the total of the payments listed shows at the top of the list.

Recording a payment

  1. Click New Payment.
  2. Under Find Student, choose the Academic Year (and Session if you like), the Class, then type a name or admission number in Student and pick the learner.
  3. Under Payment Details, choose the invoice in Apply to Invoice; each shows its balance. Leave it -- Unallocated -- only if the money is not for a particular invoice.
  4. Enter the Date, the Amount and the Method (Cash, M-Pesa, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Card or Other). Choose the Receiving Account the money went into, and type the M-Pesa code or bank slip number in Transaction Ref.
  5. Leave Receipt # blank to number the receipt automatically, or type the number of a manual receipt book.
  6. Check the Fee Category Apportionment: how the payment is shared across the invoice's fees.
  7. Click Record Payment.

How a payment is shared across fees depends on the school's setting. In Auto mode ShuleNova pays the fees in order of priority (the fee category's rank) until the money runs out. In Manual mode you type the amount for each fee, and the total must equal the payment.

Viewing a receipt

Click the eye (View) button. The receipt shows the learner, date, amount, method, invoice, receiving account and reference, who paid (the family or a sponsor) and how the money was shared across the fees.

A payment made by a sponsor shows "Paid by" the sponsor. It is part of a sponsor payment, and can only be undone by deleting that payment under Sponsors > Sponsor Payments.

The paper plane button on a receipt sends a payment acknowledgement to the learner's parents, after the same preview as for invoices.

Correcting a payment: credit and debit notes

Payments are never edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a note, which goes through approval:

  • A credit note (the minus button) reverses all or part of a payment and puts the amount back on the invoice balance, for example a payment recorded against the wrong learner. It cannot be more than the payment.
  • A debit note (the plus button) adds to the invoice total, increasing what the learner owes, for example a bounced cheque.
  1. Click the credit or debit note button on the payment.
  2. Type the Amount and the Reason (both required).
  3. Pick the approvers and click Issue & Submit for Approval.

The note appears in the payments list with its own number, type and status, and changes the invoice only when it is approved.

Fee Reports

Finance > Fees > Fee Reports has five tabs. Each opens on the current year and term already loaded; change the filters and click Load. Changing a filter clears the report until you load it again, so what is on screen always matches the filters. Print prints the report with the school's heading. The tab is kept in the page address, so a bookmark opens the same tab.

Most rows can be clicked: a class opens to its learners, and a learner opens their statement in a panel on the right, from which you can open each invoice or receipt. The panel has buttons to print or download what it shows as a CSV file.

Fee Collection

For a year, term and (optionally) a class and dates: total invoiced, collected and outstanding, how many invoices are fully paid, and each class with its collection rate. Open a class to see its learners; search for a learner, or tick Only students who owe.

Outstanding

Every learner with a balance for the year and term: the balance brought forward from before, invoiced, paid, balance, and how much of it is awaiting a sponsor's payment.

Balances by Class

The learners owing in each class, with totals. The printout has a Parent Signature column, useful for a class list to send home.

Fee reminders. On the Outstanding and Balances by Class tabs, Send reminders sends a fee balance reminder to the parents of every learner in the report, and Remind on a class sends to that class only. Each message states the learner's total balance. You see a preview before anything is sent.

Discounts & Sponsorships

Learners who have a discount or a sponsor: their fees, the discount, what sponsors pledged and have paid, and the family's own share. Filter by sponsor, or choose the school's own discounts.

Student Statement

One learner's account: balance brought forward, every invoice, receipt, sponsor payment, credit and debit note in date order, with a running balance and who received each payment. It opens on the current term, the first class and its first learner. Choose the Academic Year, Session, Class and Student, then click Load. Click a line to open the invoice or receipt.

Answering a parent at the counter. The Student Statement keeps the learner in the page address. Print it for the parent, or bookmark it.

Sponsors

A sponsor is anyone other than the family who pays a learner's fees: an NGO or company, the school's own bursary fund, the government, a relative. Open Finance > Sponsors > Sponsors.

  1. Click Add Sponsor.
  2. Type the Name and choose the Sponsor Type: External (NGO / Foundation / Company), School (Internal Bursary Fund), Government, Parent or Other.
  3. Add the Contact Name, Email, Phone and Address, and click Save.

Click a sponsor's name (or the statement button) to open their statement for the year so far, with the pledges and payments behind it.

Sponsorships

A sponsorship says what a sponsor covers, for whom and for how long. Open Finance > Sponsors > Sponsorships. The On invoices column shows how many invoices it has been applied to and the amount pledged on them.

Adding a sponsorship

  1. Click Add Sponsorship.
  2. Under Sponsor & Period, choose the Sponsor, the Academic Year, and a Session (Term) or -- Whole Year --. Add start and end dates if needed, and leave the Status Active.
  3. Under Beneficiary, choose the Target: an Individual Student (pick the class, then the student) or an Entire Class.
  4. Under Coverage, choose Full Coverage (100%), Percentage (1 to 100) or Fixed Amount. Tick the fee categories it covers, or leave them all unticked to cover every fee.
  5. Click Save.

New invoices pick up active sponsorships by themselves. For invoices already raised, click the apply button (Apply to existing open invoices) on the sponsorship: it records the sponsor's pledge on the matching lines of every open invoice, skipping fully paid invoices and lines another sponsor already covers, and puts any credit the sponsor holds onto them.

Once on invoices, a sponsorship is fixed. It can no longer be edited or deleted (a lock shows instead). To stop it, click End: no new invoice takes it from today, and the invoices it is already on stay as they are.

Sponsor Payments

When a sponsor sends money, record it once here and ShuleNova shares it across the sponsored learners' invoices. Open Finance > Sponsors > Sponsor Payments.

  1. Click Record Payment.
  2. Choose the Sponsor, and enter the Date, Amount, Method, Transaction Ref and the Bank / Cash Account it went into.
  3. Click Next: onto students' invoices. ShuleNova lists the sponsor's unpaid pledges, oldest first, and shares the payment across them. Change any amount in This payment if the sponsor said which learners it is for; Oldest first shares it again.
  4. Click Record Payment.

Each learner's share appears as a receipt under Fee Payments, "Paid by" the sponsor. Money left over is held as credit for the sponsor and goes onto their learners' invoices as new pledges fall due; the Held column shows it, and its button puts held credit onto pledges now due. Deleting a sponsor payment also takes it off the learners' invoices.

Expenses

Every payment the school makes (suppliers, utilities, repairs, allowances) is recorded as an expense and approved before it reaches the books. Open Finance > Expenses & Accounts > Expenses. The list shows the current month; change Date From and Date To to see other dates.

Recording an expense

  1. Click Add Expense.
  2. Enter the Date, the Payment Method, the account it was Paid From, the Transaction Ref and a Description. The Ref # is given automatically.
  3. Under Expense Lines, click Add Line for each item: choose the Category, type the Vendor, the Amount and a description. One expense can have several lines, for example one invoice from a supplier covering stationery and cleaning materials.
  4. Pick the approvers and click Save & Submit for Approval.

An expense is a Draft until it is fully Approved (and posted to the ledger) or Rejected. While it waits for its approvers the list still shows Draft; the edit and delete buttons disappear, and the Approval box in the expense shows which step it has reached. An approved expense cannot be edited or deleted. The eye (Quick View) button shows an expense's lines.

Journal Entries

Most entries in the books are made for you by invoices, payments, approved expenses and payroll. A journal entry is for anything else: opening balances, moving money between bank and cash, depreciation, correcting a posting. Open Finance > Expenses & Accounts > Journal Entries; the list shows the current month.

  1. Click New Entry, set the Entry Date and a Description.
  2. Click Add Line for each account: choose the Account and type a Debit or a Credit. A hint under the account shows on which side it normally increases.
  3. When total debits equal total credits, the entry shows Balanced and the save button becomes active. An entry needs at least two lines.
  4. Pick the approvers and click Save & Submit for Approval.
Submit as you save. A journal entry cannot be opened for editing once saved: there is no edit button. Pick the approvers before you save it. To change a draft entry, delete it and enter it again. Approved entries cannot be deleted.

The entry is posted to the ledger when it is approved. Entries made automatically by payments, invoices and expenses also appear here, already approved. Use the eye (Quick View) button to see its lines.

Payroll journals. Depending on the school's setting, approving a payroll can leave its journal here as a draft, with every figure filled in, for you to check, adjust and approve.

Accounting Periods

At the end of each term (or each year, as the school chose) the books are closed: the period's income less expenses is moved to Retained Earnings, and nothing dated in it can be recorded, changed or deleted any more. Open Finance > Expenses & Accounts > Accounting Periods and choose the Academic Year.

Each period shows its income, expenses and surplus, and a status: Current, Open (with the reasons it cannot close yet listed underneath), Ready to close, or Closed (with the date, who closed it and a link to the closing entry). A period cannot be closed until:

  • it has ended;
  • the period before it is closed;
  • every expense, journal entry and credit or debit note dated in it has been approved or rejected;
  • the Retained Earnings account is set in the finance settings.
  1. When the period shows Ready to close, click Close.
  2. Read the confirmation: the surplus or deficit going to Retained Earnings, and the dates that will be locked. Click Close the period.

Learners' unpaid fees are not lost: they carry into the next period as the balance brought forward, and can still be paid. If something must be corrected after closing, an administrator can Reopen the latest closed period, which takes its closing entry out; close it again when the correction is done.

"... is closed". If ShuleNova refuses a payment, invoice or expense with this message, its date falls in a closed period. Date it in the open period instead, or ask an administrator to reopen the period.

Financial Statements

Finance > Reports > Statements has four tabs: Budget, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet. Each opens loaded for this year to date; change the dates and click Load, and Print for a copy with the school's heading. Click any account to see the transactions behind its figure, and open each one from there.

Trial Balance

Every account's debit or credit total between the From and To dates. The two columns must agree.

Profit & Loss

Income and expenses by account between two dates, and the net profit (surplus) or loss.

Balance Sheet

Assets, liabilities and equity As of Date, with the total of liabilities and equity. Until the current period is closed, its surplus shows under Equity as "Surplus not yet closed"; closing the period moves it to Retained Earnings.

Budget

All departments' budgets for an academic year against what was actually received and spent: budgeted and actual income and expense, the net, and each department's lines with their variance. Click a line to see the transactions.

Expenses Report

Finance > Reports > Expenses Report has two tabs.

  • Expenses: what was spent between two dates, in total and by category, with each category's share. Open a category to see its expenses, or search by reference, vendor or description; click an expense to open it.
  • Credit/Debit Notes: the notes raised in a period, by year, term, class, type and status, with the total of each and their net effect on fees owed.
