Payroll Set-up

Before the first payroll is run, ShuleNova needs the school's pay items and the statutory rules: what staff can earn, what can be deducted, the PAYE tax bands, the NSSF tiers, the tax reliefs, and the rates for SHIF and the Housing Levy. This is done once, and then changed only when the law or the school's policy changes. The monthly payroll itself is described in the user guide.

Where it lives. The statutory set-up is under Settings > Dropdowns > Payroll Settings, because it changes by law. The school's own choices (the employer KRA PIN, net pay rounding, the payroll accounts) are under Settings > Global Settings.

Recommended Set-up Order

  1. Global Settings > Payroll Settings: the school's KRA PIN and how net pay is rounded.
  2. Global Settings > Finance Settings and Staff Categories: the accounts the payroll journal uses (only if the school keeps its accounts in ShuleNova).
  3. Earning Types and Deduction Types: the pay items.
  4. Tax Bands, NSSF Bands and Reliefs: the PAYE and NSSF rules.
  5. Rates & Limits: SHIF, the Housing Levy and the retirement relief limits.
  6. Settings > Approval Workflows: who reviews and approves the payroll.
  7. Security > Menu Permissions: give the payroll officers' role the Payroll pages.
  8. Staff records: each employee's KRA PIN, ID, NSSF and SHA numbers and bank details; then their salaries under Payroll > Employee Salaries.

Open Settings > Dropdowns and click Payroll Settings on the left. Its six links, in this order, open the set-up pages. Each page has a Back to Dropdowns button.

Payroll in Global Settings

Open Settings > Global Settings and click Payroll Settings to open the section. Change a value, then click Save changes at the top.

SettingWhat it does
School KRA PINThe school's KRA PIN as employer, printed in the payslip header and on the P9 cards. Leave blank to leave it off.

Rounding changes take effect the next time a period is processed.

The payroll accounts

When a payroll is approved, ShuleNova builds its salary journal. The Finance Settings section of Global Settings says what happens and which accounts it uses:

  • Payroll Journal: Draft for review (recommended) leaves a filled-in journal under Journal Entries for finance to check and approve; Post automatically on approval puts it straight into the ledger; Off creates nothing.
  • PAYE Payable, NSSF Payable, SHIF Payable and Housing Levy accounts; Payroll Deductions Payable for other deductions without an account of their own; Staff Loans & Advances for loan recoveries.
  • Salaries are charged to the expense account on each staff category (Settings > Dropdowns > Staff Categories), so teaching and non-teaching pay stay apart. Default Salary Expense Account is used only for a category with no account; leave it blank to have approval stop and say so instead.

If the accounts are not set up at all, payroll can still be approved; no journal is made.

Earning Types

Earning types are the things staff can be paid. Basic Salary, House Allowance and Transport Allowance are marked System: they come from the columns of the salary grid and cannot be applied from here. Add the others your school pays, such as overtime, leave, hardship, acting or responsibility allowances.

  1. Click Add earning type.
  2. Type the Name and a short Code. Tick Taxable unless the law exempts it: a non-taxable earning is still paid but left out of taxable pay.
  3. Under Calculation, choose the Method: Fixed amount or Percentage of basic salary, and the amount or percentage.
  4. Switch on Applies to all staff only for an earning everyone gets. It is then paid to everyone on the payroll without adding it to each employee; someone with their own line for it keeps their own amount. Otherwise it is added per employee on the salary.
  5. Click Save.

Untick Active to retire an earning type rather than deleting one that has been used.

Deduction Types

Deduction types are what can be taken off pay. PAYE, NSSF Employee, SHIF and Affordable Housing Levy are System: payroll works them out from the statutory rules. Add the school's others: SACCO, union dues, welfare, HELB, a pension scheme, insurance premiums.

Click Add deduction type (or the edit button) and fill in:

FieldWhat it does
Name, Code, Statutory, ActiveWhat the deduction is called and whether it is a statutory one.

Applies to all staffDeducted from everyone on the payroll without adding it to each employee. An employee with their own line keeps their own amount.

Insurance relief. A deduction that earns a tax relief, such as an insurance premium, is linked from the relief itself (see Reliefs), not here.

Tax Bands

The PAYE bands, applied in turn from the lowest: tax on the pay between each band's Lower Limit and Upper Limit at its Rate (%).

  1. Click Add tax band, type a Description (for example "Up to KES 24,000"), the Lower Limit, Upper Limit and Rate (%), and an Effective Date.
  2. Make each band's lower limit the previous band's upper limit, starting from 0. Give the top band a very large upper limit (for example 99,999,999).
Only active bands count. Every active band is used, whatever its effective date. When the law changes the bands, change the figures, or add the new bands and untick Active on the old ones, so that only one set is active.

NSSF Bands

NSSF is charged in tiers: each tier charges its rate on the pay between its two limits. The tiers sharing an effective date form one set, and each payroll month uses the most recent set in force for that month. The page shows each set with its dates, its maximum contribution a month and whether it is In use now, Not yet in force or Superseded.

  1. Click Add band. Type the Name (for example "NSSF Tier I"), the Tier number, the date it is Effective from, the Lower limit, Upper limit and Rate (%), and save.
  2. Add the next tier to the same set with the + Tier button on the set's header. Tier II's lower limit is normally Tier I's upper limit.
When the limits change. NSSF limits change most Februaries. Do not edit the old figures: add a new set dated from the day the new limits take effect. Re-processing an older month then still uses that month's limits. Payroll cannot be processed for a month with no NSSF set in force.

Reliefs

A relief comes off the tax itself, after the bands have been applied, such as personal relief and insurance relief. (A tax deductible deduction is different: it comes off income before the bands, and is set on the deduction type.)

  1. Click Add relief and type the Name and Code.
  2. Choose the Basis:
    • Fixed amount: a flat sum each month, such as personal relief. Switch on Applies to all staff to give it to everyone, or link it to a deduction under Worked out from to give it only to staff who pay that deduction.
    • Percentage of a deduction: a percentage of what the employee paid towards the deduction chosen under Worked out from, such as insurance relief on insurance premiums.
  3. Set a Monthly cap if the law limits it (leave blank for none) and save.
Personal relief. Without at least a personal relief, PAYE is charged on the full tax.

Rates & Limits

The remaining statutory figures are kept as settings, each with a Key payroll looks for, a Name and a Value. Filter by Category, and use the edit button to change a value.

KeyWhat it is
ShifRateSHIF, as a percentage of gross pay (2.75).
ShifMinimumThe smallest SHIF deduction a month.
ShifRoundingUnit1 rounds SHIF to the nearest shilling; 0 keeps the cents.
AhlRateThe Housing Levy taken from the employee, as a percentage of gross pay (1.5).
AhlEmployerRateThe school's matching Housing Levy, as a percentage of gross pay. It is a cost to the school, never deducted from pay.
RetirementReliefPercentNSSF and pension contributions come off taxable income up to this percentage of basic pay...
RetirementReliefCap...and up to this amount a month, whichever is lower. A limit of 0 is not applied.
Keep the keys exactly. Payroll reads these by their key, and only while they are active. Change the value, never the key; a setting with a mistyped key, or an inactive one, counts as 0.

Approval and Access

  • Approval. Under Settings > Approval Workflows, the Payroll workflow sets who reviews and who approves a processed payroll. While it is active with at least one step, payroll officers submit each month for approval and only the final approver's approval locks it. If it is switched off, a payroll officer approves with a single click on the Processing page.
  • Payroll officers. Give the payroll officers' role the Payroll pages under Security > Menu Permissions. Anyone holding Processing can open every employee's payslips, including ones not yet approved; everyone else sees only their own, once approved.
  • My Payslips. A member of staff sees their own payslips only when their user account is linked to their staff record.
