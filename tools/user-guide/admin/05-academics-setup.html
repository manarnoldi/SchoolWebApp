Academics Set-up

Before teachers can enter marks, plan lessons or print report forms, the school's academic structure has to be in place: the subjects and which grades take them, the grading bands, the exam types, the CBE curriculum designs, and who teaches what. This chapter is for the person who sets all that up, usually the deputy head (academics) or the director of studies. Most of it is done once and then checked at the start of each year or term.

Two kinds of "grade". In ShuleNova a grade (Grade 4, PP 1) is a learning level, a year of schooling. The grading system is the score bands (EE, ME, AE, BE) used to grade marks. This chapter uses "grade" for the first and "grading" for the second.

Set-up Order for a New Year or Term

Each step relies on the one before it, so work down this list. Steps 1 to 3 are done once when the school starts on ShuleNova and only revisited when something changes.

#StepWhere
1Lookup lists: subject groups, exam types, competencies, values, activitiesSettings > Dropdowns, Academics and CBE Settings sections
2Subjects and the grading systemSettings > Dropdowns > Academics: Subjects, Grading System
3Curriculum designs: strands, sub-strands, outcomes and the restSettings > Dropdowns > CBE Settings: Curriculum Upload (Excel)
4The academic year and its terms (sessions)School > Academic Years, School > Sessions
5The year's classesSchool > Classes
6Subjects for each education level in the new year (copy last year's)Settings > Dropdowns > Academics > Edu-Level Subjects
7Learners into their new classesStudents > Class Assignment
8Teachers to subjects in each classStaff > Subject Allocation
9Learners to their subjects, above all the optional onesStudents > Subject Allocation
10Each term's examsAcademics > Exams > School Exams, then Register Exams
11Check the grading, ranking and report-form settingsSettings > Global Settings

For a new term in the same year only step 10 is needed, plus any changes to allocations (step 8 or 9) when a teacher changes subjects or a learner drops one. The curriculum (strands and outcomes) is not tied to a year, so it carries over by itself.

The Dropdowns Page

Most of the academic lists are kept on one page, Settings > Dropdowns. The left column has a section per area. Click a section's title to open it. Click a plain list name (such as Exam Types) to see its items on the right. The blue names with an arrow icon (such as Subjects) open a page of their own. Each of those pages has a Back to Dropdowns button that returns you to the same section.

Adding or changing an item in a simple list

  1. Open the section and click the list name.
  2. Click Add … at the top of the list (for example Add Exam Types), fill in the form and click Save.
  3. To change an item, click its blue edit button; to remove one, the red delete button. Deleting cannot be undone, so change an item that is already in use rather than deleting it.

Rank sets the order an item appears in everywhere it is offered, lowest first. It is worth numbering ranks in tens (10, 20, 30) so you can slot a new item in later without renumbering.

The Academics section holds Curricula, Subject Groups and Exam Types, plus links to Subjects, Edu-Level Subjects and the Grading System. The CBE Settings section holds the CBE lists and the curriculum design pages:

The System Settings section also holds lists that academics depend on: Departments (with each department's Head of Department, see Who Sees What), Education Levels (Pre-primary, Lower primary…), Learning Levels (the grades themselves) and Streams. A learning level's Requires Teaching Documents switch takes a whole grade out of Teaching Management when it is off.

Subjects

Open Settings > Dropdowns > Academics > Subjects. The list shows every subject (learning area) with its code, the number of lessons a week, whether it is optional, its group and department.

Adding a subject

  1. Click Add subject.
  2. Fill in the form (all fields marked * are required) and click Save, then confirm.
FieldWhat it means
Subject group, Department nameWhere the subject belongs. The department decides which head of department oversees its teaching documents.
Subject name, code, abbreviationThe abbreviation is what appears in narrow columns, such as on the broadsheet.
Number of lessons/WeekThe usual number of lessons. The curriculum design can set it per grade (see Curriculum Designs).
Head of subject (HOS)Optional: the member of staff who leads the subject.
Subject optional?On for subjects only some learners take (French, a religious education). Optional subjects must be allocated to the learners who take them.
Examinable?Off for a subject that is taught but not examined.
Requires teaching documents?Schemes of work, lesson plans and record of work. Turn it off for a learning area with no curriculum design to plan from; it then stays out of Teaching Management. Such subjects show a No teaching docs tag in the list.

The Classes button on a subject's row shows, for the chosen year, every class that takes the subject, its teacher, how many learners the class has and how many are taking the subject. Its Manage allocations button opens the staff allocation page.

Subjects for each education level and year

A subject is offered to a grade only once it is listed for that grade's education level in that academic year. Open Settings > Dropdowns > Academics > Edu-Level Subjects, choose the year and education level, and click Search.

  • Add Edu-Level Subjects: choose the academic year and education level, tick the subjects, and save.
  • Copy to Year: copies the subjects listed on the screen to another academic year. At the start of a new year, open each education level for last year, click Copy to Year, pick the new year and click Copy.
Missing subject? If a subject does not appear on the staff allocation page, in exam registration or in the curriculum upload for a grade, it is almost always missing here for that year and education level.

The Grading System

Open Settings > Dropdowns > Academics > Grading System. Each row is one grade band: a mark range and the grade, points and remarks it gives. The demonstration school has both the CBE scales:

  • 4-Point: EE, ME, AE, BE, worth 4 to 1 points.
  • 8-Point: EE 1, EE 2, ME 1, ME 2, AE 1, AE 2, BE 1, BE 2, worth 8 to 1 points.

To add or change a band, click Add grade (or a row's edit button) and fill in the Curriculum, Category (4-Point or 8-Point), Grade name, Abbreviation, Minimum score and Maximum score, Points, Rank and the remarks in Swahili and English. The remarks print on report forms.

No gaps, no overlaps. The bands of one category must cover every mark from 0 to 100 exactly once (for example 0–24, 25–49, 50–74, 75–100). A mark that falls in no band gets no grade.

Which scale is used for exams, assessments, values and co-curricular scores is chosen in Global Settings.

Exam Types

Exam types are the kinds of exam the school sets. Every exam the school registers belongs to one. Manage them under Settings > Dropdowns > Academics > Exam Types (shown in The Dropdowns Page).

FieldWhat it does
Name, AbbreviationFor example End-Term Exam, END. The abbreviation heads the exam's column on the report form.
Show on report formYes: the type gets its own column on the report form. Leave it off for exams kept for internal tracking only, such as weekly tests or KPSEA and KJSEA practice.
Allow several exams per termOff (the usual case): a term can hold only one exam of this type, so each report-form column is clear. On: several in a term, such as weekly tests. Registering a second exam of a type that is off is refused, with a message saying which box to tick.
RankThe order of the columns on the report form (opening, mid-term, end-term).

Each term, the exams themselves are created under Academics > Exams > School Exams (term, exam type and mark-entry deadline) and then registered for the subjects and classes under Register Exams.

Curriculum Designs (CBE)

The KICD curriculum design of each subject and grade lives in ShuleNova. Teachers build schemes of work, lesson plans and assessments from it, so it must be loaded for every subject and grade the school teaches. A design is made up of:

PartWhat it is
ThemesOptional groupings of strands, used by some lower-grade designs.
Strands and sub-strandsThe topics and sub-topics, with their codes (1.0 Numbers, 1.1 Whole Numbers).
Specific learning outcomesWhat the learner should be able to do by the end of a sub-strand. Learners are assessed against these.
General outcomesThe outcomes for an education level as a whole.
Broad/Subject outcomesThe subject's general learning outcomes for an education level.
Learning experiences, key inquiry questionsThe suggested activities and questions for each sub-strand.
PCIsPertinent and contemporary issues linked to each sub-strand.
Lesson allocationsLessons per week for each subject in each grade. Schemes of work use it.
Rubrics, appendix, other sectionsThe assessment rubrics, the suggested resources and assessment methods, and any other section of the design word for word.

Each part has its own page under Settings > Dropdowns > CBE Settings (Strands, Sub-Strands, Specific Outcomes and so on). These pages work alike: choose the year, curriculum, grade and subject, click Search, and use Add … or the edit and delete buttons. They are handy for one small correction. To load a whole design, use the Excel upload below.

Loading a design from Excel

Open Settings > Dropdowns > CBE Settings > Curriculum Upload (Excel).

  1. Under 1. Download the template, choose the Grade, then the Subject / learning area, and click Download template. The workbook comes filled in with anything already saved for that subject and grade, so you can also use it to correct one.
  2. Fill in the workbook from the KICD design. Its Instructions sheet explains every column: a row per sub-strand with its outcomes, experiences, questions, competencies, values, PCIs and links to other learning areas, plus sheets for the rubrics, the subject outcomes, the essence statement and time allocation, the appendix and the other sections.
  3. Under 2. Upload the filled workbook, choose the file. ShuleNova checks it first and shows what it found (how many strands, sub-strands, outcomes and so on). Nothing is saved yet.
  4. Fix any problem listed in red in the workbook and choose the file again. Notes in yellow are saved anyway.
  5. When there are no problems left, click Save … curriculum. Then use Upload another for the next subject.
Subject not in the list? The upload offers only the subjects listed for the grade's education level under Edu-Level Subjects. Add it there first.

Editing a design's details

Settings > Dropdowns > CBE Settings > Curriculum Design Details edits the parts of a design that are not simple lists. Choose the Grade and the Subject / learning area, then use the tabs:

  • Design: the title, Lessons per week (this also becomes the subject's lesson allocation for the grade), the source and the essence statement.
  • Sub-strand details: for each sub-strand, the core competencies, values and links to other learning areas, each tied to an item in the matching list, and the suggested resources, assessment methods and non-formal activities.
  • Rubrics: per strand, the indicators and what Exceeds, Meets, Approaches and Below Expectations look like.
  • Appendix and Other sections: the remaining lists and text of the design.

Each tab has its own save button, and an unsaved changes tag shows until you save. View the whole design opens the read-only view that teachers see under Teaching Management > Curriculum.

Subject Allocation

Allocations say who teaches what and who learns what. They are kept per year, so they are part of every new year's set-up.

Teachers to subjects

  1. Open Staff > Subject Allocation.
  2. Choose the Academic Year and the Class. Every subject of the class's education level is listed.
  3. Pick the teacher for each subject under Assigned Teacher. Changed rows turn yellow and show Modified.
  4. Click Save All Changes, then move on to the next class.

A row shown in red, "Not a subject of this class's level", is an old allocation for a subject no longer listed for the level. Choose -- Remove allocation -- and save.

The allocation decides which classes and subjects a teacher sees when entering marks and assessments and when preparing teaching documents. A teacher with no allocation sees nothing to work on.

Learners to subjects

Open Students > Subject Allocation, choose the curriculum, year, education level and class, and search. Each learner's allocated subjects are listed. Click Add allocations to give subjects to the class's learners, or tick learners and click Delete selected to remove theirs. Allocate every subject a learner takes. When Totals & Means Basis is set to all allocated subjects, a missing allocation changes the learner's mean.

Grading and Academic Settings

Open Settings > Global Settings. Click a section to open it, change values, then click Save changes at the top. The button counts your unsaved changes, and only the changed settings are saved.

SettingWhat it decides
Exam Results4-Point or 8-Point grading for exam marks, the report form and the broadsheet.
Student Assessment, Value Scores, Co-Curricular ScoresThe scale for CBE assessments, values ratings and co-curricular scores.
Ranking MethodRank learners by Mean Marks (Average %) or Mean Points (Score) on the broadsheet and report form.
Class Performance Ranking BasisRank classes against others of the same education level type (all of Primary) or the same education level (Upper primary only).
Totals & Means BasisDivide a learner's total by the subjects with marks, or by all subjects allocated to them (a missing mark counts as zero).
Combined Broadsheet ColumnsMarks, grade and points per subject, or marks and grade only.
Top Students ShownHow many tied top learners the Subject and Class Performance reports list before "+N more".
Exam Results / Ranking Method – education levelOne pair per education level. Leave them on Use global default, or choose a different scale or ranking for that level only (for example 8-Point for Junior School).

Other sections hold related settings:

  • General Dashboard Settings: Current Exam Type for Dashboard (the exam type the dashboard's Class Exam Performance card opens on, usually the latest one this term) and Average Calculation Method (whether class averages count only learners who sat the exam or every allocated learner, absent ones as 0).
  • Teaching Management Settings: when schemes of work and lesson plans fall due. Only the tracker's "overdue" marking uses these.
  • Report Form Settings: what the report form shows (marks, grades, points), which learner development sections print, the position, the title wording and the term dates.
Change grading between terms, not during one. Grading and ranking are worked out when reports are produced, so a change affects every report printed afterwards, including those for earlier exams.

Who Sees What: Academic Permissions

For assessments and teaching documents, teachers see only the classes and subjects allocated to them. Three things widen that, set by an administrator.

Student Assessments (Admin)

A role with this permission can enter and correct Student Assessment marks for any teacher, class and subject, and sees the whole school on the Assessment Tracker. Administrators have it already. Give it to the role used by, for example, the deputy head (academics) or the examinations officer.

Whole-school Access (Admin)

A role with this permission sees and opens every teacher's schemes of work, lesson plans and records of work, and checks them, as administrators do. It also gives the whole school on the Assessment Tracker.

Granting either permission

  1. Open Security > Menu Permissions and click the role on the left.
  2. Under Academics > Assessments, tick Student Assessments (Admin); under Teaching Management, tick Whole-school Access (Admin).
  3. Click Save. Members of the role who are signed in get the new access when they refresh the page or sign in again.

These two entries are permissions, not pages: they never appear in the menu.

Heads of Department

A head of department sees and checks the teaching documents of every subject in their department, and their department's progress on the Assessment Tracker, with no extra permission. To set one, open Settings > Dropdowns > System Settings > Departments, edit the department and choose the staff member under Head of Department. A subject belongs to the department chosen on the subject (see Subjects).

The HeadOfDepartment role. The role of that name only decides which menus its members see. What a head of department oversees comes from the Departments list above, so set both.
