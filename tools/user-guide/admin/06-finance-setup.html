Finance Set-up

Before the bursar can raise an invoice or record an expense, ShuleNova needs the school's books set up: the chart of accounts, the fee and expense categories that point into it, the finance settings that say which accounts the automatic entries use, and the approval workflows. This chapter is for the administrator who does that, usually with the bursar beside them. Most of it is done once, when the school starts on ShuleNova; after that, the main yearly job is closing the accounting periods.

Ask the accountant. The chart of accounts is the school's accounting structure. Agree the account codes and names with the school's accountant or auditor before you enter them; changing them after a term's transactions have been posted is much harder.

Set-up Order for a New School

Each step relies on the one before it, so work down this list.

Give the bursar and accounts clerks a role that opens the Finance menu, and the approvers the roles named in the workflows (see Users).

Chart of Accounts

Every amount ShuleNova posts lands in an account. Open Settings > Dropdowns, choose the Finance section on the left and click Chart of Accounts; the same list is also at /finance/accounts, with filters for account type and status.

Each account has a Code, a Name, a Type, an optional Parent Account (to group, for example, several bank accounts under one heading) and a Description. Untick Active to stop an account being offered on forms without losing its history.

TypeUsual codesExamples a school needs
Asset1000sCash on Hand, Bank - Main Account, Bank - M-Pesa, Accounts Receivable (Fees), Staff Loans & Advances, furniture, buildings
Liability2000sAccounts Payable, Sponsor Advances, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy and other payroll deductions payable
Equity3000sRetained Earnings
Income4000sTuition, transport, meals, boarding and activity fee income, uniform sales
Expense5000sTeaching and non-teaching salaries, electricity, water, internet, stationery, cleaning, repairs, fuel, food
One bank account per real account. Make an asset account for every bank account, M-Pesa till or paybill and cash box the school takes money into. The bursar chooses one as the Receiving Account on each payment, so the trial balance shows each one's balance, ready to compare with the bank statement.

Fee Categories

A fee category is a kind of fee: Tuition, Transport, Meals, Boarding, Activity, Uniform, Exam. Fee structures and invoices are made of them. Open Settings > Dropdowns > Finance > Fee Categories (also at /finance/fee-categories).

  • Name: as parents will see it on invoices.
  • Income Account: the income account its fees are posted to. Several categories may share one (an exam fee posted to tuition income, for example).
  • Rank: the order in which a payment pays the fees off when the school uses automatic apportioning. Rank 1 is paid first. Put the fees the school most needs collected first at the top.
  • Active: untick a category no longer charged.
Set the income account. The income journal of an invoice leaves out any fee whose category has no income account, so that income never reaches the books. Check every category has one before the first invoices are raised.

Expense Categories

An expense category is what the bursar chooses on each line of an expense: Electricity, Stationery, Repairs, Food Supplies and so on. Each points to the expense account it is posted to. Open Settings > Dropdowns > Finance > Expense Categories (also at /finance/expense-categories) and give each a Name, an Expense Account, a Rank (the order they are listed in) and a description.

Keep the list short and clear: an expense category that matches an account one to one makes the Expenses Report and the Profit & Loss agree line for line.

Finance Settings

Open Settings > Global Settings and click Finance Settings. Change what you need, then click Save changes at the top; the page counts the unsaved changes until you do.

SettingWhat to choose
Accounting PeriodTermly closes the books at the end of each term, Yearly at the end of each academic year. A term runs from its first day to the day before the next term starts, so the holiday after it belongs to it.
Retained Earnings AccountThe equity account each closed period's surplus is moved to. A period cannot be closed until this is set.
Payment Apportioning ModeAuto shares each payment across the invoice's fees by fee category rank. Manual makes the clerk type the amount for each fee.
Auto-Post Invoice JournalOn: each invoice posts Debit Debtors, Credit the fee's income account. Leave it on.
Auto-Post Payment JournalOn: each payment posts Debit the receiving bank or cash account, Credit Debtors. Leave it on.
Student Debtors AccountThe asset account for what learners owe (for example 1200 Accounts Receivable (Fees)).
Default Cash/Bank AccountThe asset account used when a payment's Receiving Account, or an expense's Paid From account, is left blank.
Sponsor Advances AccountThe liability account that holds sponsor money not yet put onto learners' invoices.

The same section holds the payroll accounts: the Payroll Journal mode (a draft journal for the bursar to check, posting automatically on approval, or off), the fallback salary expense account, the PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy and other deductions payable accounts, and the staff loans account. They are covered in the Payroll Set-up chapter.

Leave auto-posting on. With both auto-post settings on, the trial balance, profit & loss and balance sheet always match the fee records. Switch them off only if the accountant keeps the books elsewhere and posts journals by hand.

Approval Workflows for Finance

Expenses, journal entries, credit and debit notes and budget amendments are posted only when the last approver approves them. Each of these forms needs an active workflow under Settings > Approval Workflows (see Approval Workflows): without one, the form shows "No active workflow configured" and nothing can be approved. A common choice is two steps, the bursar or accountant as Reviewer and the head teacher as Approver, with maker-checker on so that nobody approves their own expense.

Approved records are locked. Only a SuperAdministrator can reverse an approval, which puts the record back to draft.

Closing and Reopening Periods

The bursar closes each period under Finance > Expenses & Accounts > Accounting Periods once it has ended and everything in it is approved (see the User Guide). Closing moves the surplus to Retained Earnings and locks every date in the period: no payment, invoice, expense or journal dated in it can be added, changed or deleted.

When a mistake in a closed period must be corrected, an Administrator or SuperAdministrator can click Reopen. Only the latest closed period can be reopened; its closing entry is taken out, the correction can be made, and the period is then closed again. Reopen only with the accountant's agreement: reports already given to the board for that period will change.

Close in order. A period cannot close while the one before it is still open, and a closed period can only be reopened after every later one has been reopened.
