Introduction for Administrators

This guide is for the person who sets up and runs ShuleNova for a school: the system administrator. It covers what only an administrator does: the school's structure (years, terms, classes), user accounts and what each role may open, the settings that change how the system behaves, and the licence. Day-to-day work (marking a register, recording a payment, printing a report form) is in the User Guide, which every user can open.

Work on the live site with care. Everything you change here affects every user at once, and every change is recorded in the audit trail under your name. Where a page asks you to confirm, read the message before you click.

What the Administrator Does

  • Sets up the school: its details and logo, the academic years and terms, the classes and streams, the calendar of events, and the lists that fill the drop-down boxes all over the system.
  • Manages people's access: creates a user account for each member of staff (and, if the school wants, for parents and learners), gives each account its roles, resets forgotten passwords and removes accounts that are no longer needed.
  • Decides what each role sees: which menus and pages each role can open, and who approves expenses, budgets, schemes of work and other requests.
  • Keeps an eye on the system: who is signed in, what has been changed and by whom, and any errors the system has recorded.
  • Looks after the licence: checks when it expires and installs a new key from Kodetek when the school renews or adds a module.

Administrator, SuperAdministrator and Other Roles

Every user account holds one or more roles. A role is a name (Teacher, Accounts, HeadTeacher…) with a set of menus attached to it. A user with several roles sees everything each of their roles sees.

RoleWhat it means
AdministratorManages users and roles, menu permissions, approval workflows, the logs and the licence, and opens the administrator guide. What an Administrator sees in the side menu still follows the menus ticked for the Administrator role in Security > Menu Permissions.
SuperAdministratorSees every menu, whatever Menu Permissions say. Only a SuperAdministrator can give the SuperAdministrator role to someone or take it away, set up the SMS gateway, and reverse a request that has already been approved. Usually held by Kodetek's support account and at most one senior person at the school. Give it together with Administrator.
ViewerView-only. Given on top of another role, the user sees what that role sees and can change nothing. For board members, auditors or a demonstration login. See The Viewer Role.
Other rolesTeacher, HeadTeacher, DeputyHeadTeacher, HeadOfDepartment, Accounts and the rest. Each sees the menus ticked for it in Menu Permissions. You can add roles of your own.
Keep the Administrator role to a few people. An Administrator can create accounts and change anyone's access. Give it to the one or two people who run the system, not to every head of section.

Setting Up a New School: the Order to Follow

Much of ShuleNova depends on things set up before it: a class needs a year and a learning level, a learner needs a class, a mark needs a subject. Setting things up in this order saves going back.

#WhatWhere
1School name, logo, contacts, motto and report headingsSchool > Details (School Details)
2Curricula, education level types, education levels, learning levels (the grades) and streamsSettings > Dropdowns (Dropdowns)
3The academic yearSchool > Academic Years (Academic Years)
4The year's terms with their datesSchool > Sessions (Sessions)
5The year's classes (grade + stream)School > Classes (Classes)
6The general lists: genders, religions, relationships, designations, staff categories, departments and the restSettings > Dropdowns > System Settings
7Staff records, then a user account and roles for eachStaff > Staff Details, then Security > Users (Users)
8What each role may openSecurity > Menu Permissions (Menu Permissions)
9Learners and their parents, placed in their classesStudents > Basic Details and Students > Parents (see the User Guide)
10Global settings and approval workflowsSettings > Global Settings, Settings > Approval Workflows (Global Settings)
11Each module the school has boughtThe later chapters of this guide

Then, for step 11:

  • Academics: subjects, the grading system, exam types, the CBE curriculum designs and subject allocation. See the Academics Set-up chapter.
  • Finance: the chart of accounts, fee and expense categories, fee structures and the finance settings. See the Finance chapter.
  • Payroll: earning and deduction types, tax and NSSF bands, reliefs, and each employee's salary. See the Payroll chapter.
  • Elections: the elections settings, then each election's posts and polling stations. See the Elections chapter.
  • Communications: the SMS gateway (by Kodetek or a SuperAdministrator), message templates and where parents' numbers come from. See the Communications chapter.
Each new year. At the start of every year you repeat steps 3 to 5 (a new year, its terms and its classes; the Classes page can copy last year's classes for you), then move learners up with Students > Class Assignment.

The Licence

ShuleNova is sold as a core system plus paid modules. The core (dashboard, school, students, staff, settings, reports and security) is always included. The paid modules are:

  • Academics (with Teaching Management): assessments, exams, report forms, schemes of work, lesson plans and records of work.
  • Finance: budgets, fees, invoices, payments, sponsors, expenses and accounts.
  • Payroll: salaries, loans, payroll processing and payslips.
  • Elections: student elections, polling stations and results.
  • Communications: SMS and email messages to parents and staff.

The licence key Kodetek installs for the school says which modules it includes and until when. A module that is not licensed simply does not appear: its menus, its dashboard cards, its sections in Settings and its chapters in these guides are all hidden. Its data is not deleted. The Licence explains how to read the licence and install a new key.

How These Guides Work

The two book icons at the top right of every page open the guides:

  • The User Guide is for everyone who signs in.
  • The Administrator Guide (this one) shows only to users holding the Administrator or SuperAdministrator role.
  • Both show only the chapters and sections for the modules your licence includes, so they always match what the school can use.

To hide both guides from everyone (for example on a demonstration site), switch off Settings > Global Settings > Security Settings > Show user guides and save. The icons are gone for each user the next time they sign in or refresh the page.
